# Dr. Sayed Abortion Questions (2026)

### Q1: In therapeutic (legal) abortion, all of the following conditions must be fulfilled EXCEPT:
- **A)** The medical practitioner must consult another specialist and obtain written concurrence
- **B)** Obtain only an informal oral consent from the husband
- **C)** Maintain complete, adequate clinical records and reports about the case
- **D)** The abortion must be performed in an authorized hospital facility

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Therapeutic abortion strictly requires informed, written consent from both the woman and her husband; oral consent is legally insufficient.

### Q2: Regarding methods used in criminal abortion:
- **A)** Local violence is the most dangerous method
- **B)** General violence is the least dangerous method
- **C)** General violence succeeds only in susceptible, predisposed women
- **D)** All of the above

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Local violence carries high risk of uterine perforation, hemorrhage, and fatal sepsis; general violence (exercise, hot baths, abdominal trauma) rarely succeeds except in women already prone to spontaneous abortion.

---

### Q3: The following are immediate causes of death in criminal abortion EXCEPT:
- **A)** Massive hemorrhage
- **B)** Venous air embolism
- **C)** Reflex vagal inhibition of the heart
- **D)** Septic complications from unsterile instrumentation

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Sepsis, septicemia, and peritonitis from unsterile instruments develop as delayed causes of death (after 24-48 hours), whereas vagal shock, air embolism, and severe hemorrhage occur immediately during the procedure.

### Q4: All of the following abortifacient drugs are classified as direct uterine ecbolics EXCEPT:
- **A)** Oxytocin
- **B)** Quinine
- **C)** Ergotamine
- **D)** Colocynth
- **E)** Oil of savin

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Colocynth is a drastic gastrointestinal purgative causing indirect pelvic congestion, whereas oxytocin, quinine, and ergotamine directly stimulate uterine smooth muscle contraction (ecbolics).

### Q5: Locally induced mechanical abortion can be performed by all of the following EXCEPT:
- **A)** Uterine syringing
- **B)** Electricity / galvanic current
- **C)** Dilatation and curettage (D&C)
- **D)** Intravenous oxytocin infusion
- **E)** Introduction of slippery elm bark

**Correct Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Oxytocin infusion is a systemic pharmacological ecbolic, not a local mechanical or instrumentation method.

---

### Q6: All of the following substances used as abortifacients are drastic purgatives EXCEPT:
- **A)** Colocynth
- **B)** Castor oil
- **C)** Cantharides
- **D)** Jalap root
- **E)** Croton oil

**Correct Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Cantharides ('Spanish fly') is a genitourinary irritant and capillary poison, not a gastrointestinal purgative like colocynth, jalap, and croton oil.

### Q7: All of the following methods of criminal abortion are commonly used by non-medical personnel EXCEPT:
- **A)** Slippery elm bark
- **B)** Knitting needles and wires
- **C)** Foreign body insertion into the cervix
- **D)** Crude electrical shocks
- **E)** Surgical Dilatation and Curettage (D&C)

**Correct Answer:** E
**Explanation:** Dilatation and Curettage (D&C) requires specialized surgical instruments (speculum, dilators, curettes) and anatomical skill, hence it is performed by medically qualified abortionists.
