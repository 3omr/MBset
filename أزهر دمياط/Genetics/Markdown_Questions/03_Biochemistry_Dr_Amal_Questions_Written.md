# Biochemistry Dr. Amal Questions (Written)

### Q1: Define DNA repair and its importance

**Model Answer:**
**Definition:** DNA repair is the cellular response to DNA damage that results in the restoration of the normal nucleotide sequence and normal DNA structure.
**Importance:** DNA is the storehouse of hereditary information and the essential blueprint for all cellular functions and genomic integrity.

---

### Q2: Enumerate 4 types of DNA repair

**Model Answer:**
1. Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER)
2. Base Excision Repair (BER)
3. Mismatch Repair (MMR)
4. Double-Strand Break Repair (Non-Homologous End Joining & Homologous Recombination)

---

### Q3: Enumerate 4 steps involved in any type of DNA repair

**Model Answer:**
1. Recognition of DNA damage
2. Removal of damaged DNA
3. Gap filling (synthesis by DNA polymerase)
4. Ligation (sealing nick by DNA ligase)

---

### Q4: Explain the molecular defect in Xeroderma Pigmentosum

**Model Answer:**
Caused by inherited deficiency/absence of UV-specific endonuclease (excinuclease), impairing the Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER) pathway. This results in failure to remove UV-induced thymine-thymine dimers, leading to accumulation of DNA mutations, severe photosensitivity, and early development of skin malignancies.
