# Final 2021 Exam Questions (MCQ)

### Q1: A purine nucleotide is:
- **A)** AMP
- **B)** UMP
- **C)** CMP
- **D)** TMP

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q2: Adjacent nucleotides are joined by:
- **A)** Covalent bond
- **B)** Phosphodiester bond
- **C)** Hydrogen bond
- **D)** Peptide bond

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q3: Telomerase acts as a reverse transcriptase which:
- **A)** Can produce DNA using RNA template
- **B)** Can produce RNA using DNA template
- **C)** Can produce protein using RNA template
- **D)** None of the above

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q4: RNA primer is formed by the enzyme:
- **A)** Ribonuclease
- **B)** Primase
- **C)** DNA polymerase
- **D)** Topoisomerase

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q5: DNA rich in G-C pairs have:
- **A)** 1 hydrogen bond
- **B)** 2 hydrogen bonds
- **C)** 3 hydrogen bonds
- **D)** 4 hydrogen bonds

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q6: The enzyme that acts to prevent extreme supercoiling of parental helix that would result as consequence of unwinding at replication fork is called:
- **A)** Helicase
- **B)** RNA polymerase
- **C)** Topoisomerase
- **D)** DNA polymerase

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q7: The poly(A) tail is attached to the hydroxyl group at:
- **A)** The 3' end of the mRNA
- **B)** The 5' end of the mRNA
- **C)** The 3' end of the tRNA
- **D)** The 5' end of the rRNA

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q8: Mutation in sickle cell anemia (RBCs contain Hb S):
- **A)** Silent mutation
- **B)** Nonsense mutation
- **C)** Missense mutation
- **D)** Frameshift mutation

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q9: The genetic code is degenerate because:
- **A)** It can undergo mutations
- **B)** A large proportion of DNA is non-coding
- **C)** One codon can code for more than one amino acid
- **D)** More than one codon can code for the same amino acid

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q10: During translation, the role of the enzyme peptidyl transferase is:
- **A)** Transfer of phosphate group
- **B)** Amino acid activation
- **C)** Peptide bond formation between adjacent amino acids
- **D)** Binding of ribosome subunits to mRNA

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q11: The enzyme aminoacyl tRNA synthetase is involved in:
- **A)** Charging of tRNA with specific amino acids
- **B)** Dissociation of discharged tRNA from 80S ribosome
- **C)** Termination of protein synthesis
- **D)** Nucleophilic attack on esterified carboxyl group of peptidyl tRNA

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q12: The binding site for the repressor protein on the lac operon is:
- **A)** The promoter
- **B)** The operator
- **C)** The corepressor
- **D)** RNA polymerase

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q13: Which of the following is NOT a non-coding RNA:
- **A)** mRNA
- **B)** tRNA
- **C)** rRNA
- **D)** miRNA

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q14: P53 gene:
- **A)** A proto-oncogene
- **B)** An oncogene
- **C)** A tumor suppressor gene
- **D)** None of these

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q15: DNA polymerase of T. aquaticus is preferred to that of E. coli in PCR because:
- **A)** It replicates DNA more accurately
- **B)** It doesn't require primers
- **C)** It is not denatured at the melting temperature of DNA
- **D)** It doesn't cause errors in replication

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q16: What is the role of the spindle fibers during mitosis?
- **A)** They help separate the chromosomes
- **B)** They break down the nuclear membrane
- **C)** They duplicate the DNA
- **D)** They make the chromosomes visible

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q17: Male and female gametes are produced by the process of:
- **A)** Mitosis
- **B)** Meiosis
- **C)** Crossing-over
- **D)** Replication

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q18: The process where the cytoplasm divides occurs in:
- **A)** Anaphase
- **B)** Telophase
- **C)** Metaphase
- **D)** Prophase

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q19: The synthesis (S) phase is characterized by:
- **A)** Cell division
- **B)** The division of cytoplasm
- **C)** DNA replication
- **D)** Replication of mitochondria and other organelles

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q20: As regard karyotyping all of the following are true EXCEPT:
- **A)** A sample of blood is collected then add anticoagulant as heparin
- **B)** Mononuclear cells are purified by centrifugation
- **C)** Cultured for 3-4 days in the presence of phytohemagglutinin to stimulate lymphocytes to divide
- **D)** Adding colchicine to interfere with spindle formation and permit intracellular accumulation of interphase

**Correct Answer:** D
