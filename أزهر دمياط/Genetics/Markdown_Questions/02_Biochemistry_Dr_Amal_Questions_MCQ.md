# Biochemistry Dr. Amal Questions (MCQ)

### Q1: Oxidative deamination is an example of:
- **A)** Exogenous DNA damage
- **B)** Endogenous DNA damage
- **C)** Replication-independent repair
- **D)** Error-prone repair

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q2: UV radiation typically causes which DNA lesion?

- **C)** Double-strand breaks
