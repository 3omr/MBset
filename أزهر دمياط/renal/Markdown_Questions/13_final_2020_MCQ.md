### Question 1

Intravenous furosemide is used for rapid control of symptoms in acute left ventricular failure. It provides quick relief of dyspnea by:

- **A)** Producing bronchodilation
- **B)** Causing rapid diuresis and reducing circulating blood volume
- **C)** Causing venodilation
- **D)** Stimulating left ventricular contractility

**Correct Answer**: C

### Question 8

A 42-year-old woman experiencing peripheral edema is referred to the nephrologist for evaluation of renal function. The patient was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes mellitus at the age of 12. Over the past 5 years, the blood glucose has not been as well managed, and 2 years ago urinalysis showed albuminuria. Investigation showed that fasting blood glucose is 300 mg/dl and plasma albumin is 1.5 g/dl. In which of the following parts of renal tubule glucose is reabsorbed?

- **A)** Initial part of distal convoluted tubule
- **B)** Late part of distal convoluted tubule
- **C)** Collecting tubule
- **D)** Proximal convoluted tubule

**Correct Answer**: D

### Question 11

A healthy 59-year-old woman ingested 1 L of water over a 20-minute period. Her urine flow rate increased after 30 minutes and remained elevated for 3 hours. Which of the following changes in her renal function can account for increased water excretion?

- **B)** End proximal tubule fluid became dilute
