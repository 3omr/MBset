### Question 1

Select the incorrect statement about the kidneys.

- **A)** They are major excretory organs of the body
- **B)** They lie on the posterior abdominal wall opposite T12-L3 vertebrae
- **C)** Their upper poles are about 5 cm away from the midline
- **D)** Their hila are crossed by the transpyloric plane

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The upper poles of the kidneys lie approximately 2.5 cm (1 inch) away from the midline, whereas the lower poles are about 7.5 cm (3 inches) away.

### Question 2

One of the following muscles is not related to the posterior surface of the kidney:

- **A)** Transversus abdominis
- **B)** Internal oblique
- **C)** Psoas major
- **D)** Quadratus lumborum

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The posterior surface of the kidney is related to four muscles: Diaphragm, Psoas major, Quadratus lumborum, and Transversus abdominis. The internal oblique muscle lies more anteriorly/laterally in the abdominal wall layers.

### Question 4

- **A)** T9-T12
- **B)** T10-L1
- **C)** T11-L2
- **D)** T12-L3

### Question 7

Select the incorrect statement about renal pelvis:

- **A)** It is the upper expanded funnel-shaped part of the ureter
- **B)** It receives major calyces
- **C)** It lies anterior to renal vessels
- **D)** It develops from the ureteric bud

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: At the renal hilum, the anatomical arrangement from anterior to posterior is: Renal Vein, Renal Artery, and Renal Pelvis (V-A-P). Therefore, the renal pelvis lies *posterior* to the renal vessels.
