### Question 3

Describe the structure of the renal corpuscle.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. A rounded structure consisting of the glomerulus and the Bowman’s capsule. b. Each one has two poles: the vascular and the urinary.

### Question 4

Explain the structure of podocytes.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. Modified simple squamous epithelium in the visceral layer of the Bowman’s capsule. b. Gives rise to primary processes and secondary processes (or pedicles, which cover much of the capillary surface). c. Contain actin microfilaments, making them contractile.

### Question 5

Define the renal filtration barrier and describe its components.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. It is the barrier that separates the blood inside the glomerular capillaries from the glomerular filtrate inside Bowman’s space. b. Components: i. Fenestrated endothelium of the glomerular capillaries. ii. Basal lamina (lamina rara and lamina densa). iii. Filtration slit diaphragm bridging the filtration slits between adjacent pedicels.

### Question 9

Discuss the structure of the juxtaglomerular apparatus.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. Juxtaglomerular (JG) cells: i. Modified smooth muscle cells mainly in the afferent arteriole but few in the efferent. ii. Show the following changes: rounded nuclei and PAS-positive secretory granules (renin). b. Extraglomerular mesangial cells (Lacis cells). c. Macula densa: i. Modified cells of DCT. ii. Show the following changes: columnar cells, apical nuclei, basal Golgi complexes, and no basement membrane.

### Question 10

Describe the structure of the urinary bladder.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. Mucosa: transitional epithelium and CT corium (or lamina propria). b. Musculosa: inner longitudinal, middle circular, and outer longitudinal smooth muscles. c. Serous layer (superior surface covered by mesothelium of peritoneum) or adventitia (inferior part) of fibroelastic CT carrying blood vessels, nerves, and lymphatics.

---

### Question 11

Describe the histological structure of the ureter.

**Type**: QROC
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: a. Mucosa = urinary bladder (transitional epithelium and lamina propria) + star-shaped lumen. b. Musculosa: i. Middle and lower parts = urinary bladder (inner longitudinal, middle circular, and outer longitudinal smooth muscles). ii. Upper part contains only two layers in the musculosa (inner longitudinal and outer circular). c. Adventitia = urinary bladder (of fibroelastic CT carrying blood vessels, nerves, and lymphatics).
