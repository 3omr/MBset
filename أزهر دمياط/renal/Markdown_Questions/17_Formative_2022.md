### Question 8

Antidiuretic hormone:

- **A)** Is secreted by hypothalamus
- **B)** Its secretion is increased when osmolality of plasma decreases
- **C)** Increases obligatory reabsorption of water
- **D)** Acts on distal convoluted tubules and collecting ducts.

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: A is wrong because it's synthesized by hypothalamus but secreted by posterior pituitary gland.
