## Section 1: Gross Anatomy

#### 1. Select the incorrect statement about the kidneys.
- [ ] a. They are major excretory organs of the body
- [ ] b. They lie on the posterior abdominal wall opposite T12-L3 vertebrae
- [x] c. Their upper poles are about 5 cm away from the midline
- [ ] d. Their hila are crossed by the transpyloric plane

> **Explanation:** The upper poles of the kidneys lie approximately 2.5 cm (1 inch) away from the midline, whereas the lower poles are about 7.5 cm (3 inches) away.

---

#### 2. One of the following muscles is not related to the posterior surface of the kidney:
- [ ] a. Transversus abdominis
- [x] b. Internal oblique
- [ ] c. Psoas major
- [ ] d. Quadratus lumborum

> **Explanation:** The posterior surface of the kidney is related to four muscles: Diaphragm, Psoas major, Quadratus lumborum, and Transversus abdominis. The internal oblique muscle lies more anteriorly/laterally in the abdominal wall layers.

---

#### 3. One of the following is not related to the anterior surface of the left kidney:
- [ ] a. Stomach
- [ ] b. Pancreas
- [ ] c. Left colic flexure
- [x] d. Left crus of diaphragm

> **Explanation:** The left crus of the diaphragm is located posteriorly, acting as part of the posterior relation of the upper pole of the left kidney.

---

#### 4. In renal colic, the pain is referred from the loin to groin mainly through the spinal segments:
- [ ] a. T9-T12
- [x] b. T10-L1
- [ ] c. T11-L2
- [ ] d. T12-L3

> **Explanation:** Renal colic pain originates from distension of the renal pelvis or ureter and is referred along the sympathetic fibers (T10–L1) to the dermatomes covering the loin, groin, and scrotum/labium majus.

---

#### 5. The length of each ureter is about:
- [ ] a. 15 cm
- [ ] b. 20 cm
- [x] c. 25 cm
- [ ] d. 30 cm

> **Explanation:** The human ureter is approximately 25 cm (10 inches) long.

---

#### 6. Select the incorrect statement about anterior relations of the left ureter:
- [ ] a. Left colic vessels
- [x] b. Ileocolic vessels
- [ ] c. Left testicular/ovarian vessels
- [ ] d. Sigmoid mesocolon

> **Explanation:** The ileocolic vessels cross anterior to the *right* ureter, not the left ureter.

---

#### 7. Select the incorrect statement about renal pelvis:
- [ ] a. It is the upper expanded funnel-shaped part of the ureter
- [ ] b. It receives major calyces
- [x] c. It lies anterior to renal vessels
- [ ] d. It develops from the ureteric bud

> **Explanation:** At the renal hilum, the anatomical arrangement from anterior to posterior is: Renal Vein, Renal Artery, and Renal Pelvis (V-A-P). Therefore, the renal pelvis lies *posterior* to the renal vessels.

---

#### 8. Select the incorrect statement about the urinary bladder:
- [ ] a. Its neck is its most fixed part
- [x] b. Its superior surface is the most movable part
- [ ] c. Its trigone is derived from the absorbed parts of the mesonephric ducts
- [ ] d. Its position is abdominopelvic at birth

> **Explanation:** While the superior surface expands freely as the bladder fills, the apex and superior peritoneal surface adjust, but statement b is classically designated as the incorrect distractor compared to fixed pelvic attachments.

---

#### 9. The bladder structure which serves as a guide to locate the ureteric orifice is:
- [x] a. Interureteric crest
- [ ] b. Uvula vesicae
- [ ] c. Trigone
- [ ] d. Uretero-urethral ridge

> **Explanation:** The interureteric crest (a transverse ridge between the two ureteric orifices) forms the superior boundary of the trigone and serves as a key landmark during cystoscopy to locate the ureteric openings.

---

#### 10. The female bladder is supplied by all arteries except:
- [ ] a. Superior vesical
- [x] b. Inferior vesical
- [ ] c. Uterine
- [ ] d. Vaginal

> **Explanation:** The inferior vesical artery is present only in males. In females, it is functionally replaced by the vaginal artery.

---

#### 11. The length of the male urethra is about:
- [ ] a. 10 cm
- [ ] b. 12 cm
- [ ] c. 16 cm
- [x] d. 20 cm

> **Explanation:** The adult male urethra measures approximately 18 to 20 cm (8 inches) in length.

---

#### 12. One of the following does not open into the posterior wall of the prostatic urethra:
- [ ] a. Prostatic utricle
- [x] b. Lacunae of Morgagni
- [ ] c. Ejaculatory ducts
- [ ] d. Prostatic glands

> **Explanation:** Lacunae of Morgagni (and urethral glands of Littré) are small mucosal recesses located in the spongy (penile) urethra, not the prostatic urethra.

---

#### 13. Select the incorrect statement about the prostatic part of urethra:
- [ ] a. It is about 3 cm in length
- [ ] b. It is the widest and most dilatable part
- [x] c. Its lumen is lined by the stratified squamous epithelium
- [ ] d. It receives the openings of ejaculatory ducts

> **Explanation:** The prostatic urethra is lined by urothelium (transitional epithelium), not stratified squamous epithelium.

---

#### 14. Select the incorrect statement about the female urethra:

- [x] b. It is embedded in the posterior wall of the vagina
- [ ] c. It pierces the perineal membrane

#### 15. All the structures open in the female urethra except:
- [ ] a. Lacunae of Morgagni
- [ ] b. Urethral glands
- [x] c. Bartholin's glands

> **Explanation:** Greater vestibular glands (Bartholin's glands) open into the vestibule of the vagina posterior to the vaginal orifice, not into the urethra.

---

#### 16. About the female urethra, one is wrong:

- [ ] a. The genital ridge
- [ ] b. Nephrogenic cord
- [ ] c. The paramesonephric duct
- [x] d. All of the above

> **Explanation:** The urogenital ridge contains both the nephrogenic cord (which forms renal structures) and the genital ridge (which forms gonads), along with developing ducts.

#### 19. Development of kidney passes through the following stages:
- [ ] a. Pronephros
- [ ] b. Mesonephros
- [ ] c. Metanephros

> **Explanation:** Human kidney development progresses sequentially through three developmental systems: Pronephros (rudimentary), Mesonephros (interim), and Metanephros (permanent kidney).
