### Q1: Describe the histological layers of the heart wall.

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
a. Endocardium:
i.
Endothelium: simple squamous epithelium.
ii.
Subendothelium: dense CT rich in elastic fibers.
iii.
Subendocardium: loose CT containing blood vessels and Purkinje
conducting fibers.
b. Myocardium: thickest layer containing endo- and perimysium.
c. Epicardium: The visceral layer of serous pericardium, i.e., simple squamous
epithelium (mesothelium), contains coronary blood vessels, nerves, and adipose
tissue.
