### Q8: Compare between selective, non-selective BB?
**Clinical Case:** **A 4 years-old female infant is brought to the pediatric clinic with a bright red, raised lesion over the left cheek that has been increasing in size since birth. The lesion is soft, non-tender, and blanches on pressure. There is no history of trauma or bleeding.** **The diagnosis of infantile hemangioma is made. Because the lesion is rapidly growing and causing facial disfigurement, oral propranolol is planned.**

**Model Answer:**
Selective β-blockers (β1 selective):** Act mainly on β1 receptors → ↓ heart rate and contractility. Examples: Atenolol, Metoprolol.
Non-selective β-blockers:** Block both β1 (heart) and β2 (lungs, vessels) → May cause bronchoconstriction. Example: Propranolol, Timolol.

### Q9: Enumerate precautions during the use of BB?
**Clinical Case:** **A 4 years-old female infant is brought to the pediatric clinic with a bright red, raised lesion over the left cheek that has been increasing in size since birth. The lesion is soft, non-tender, and blanches on pressure. There is no history of trauma or bleeding.** **The diagnosis of infantile hemangioma is made. Because the lesion is rapidly growing and causing facial disfigurement, oral propranolol is planned.**

**Model Answer:**
1. Avoid it in all contraindications.
2. If used in GIT bleeding with cirrhosis, dose of B.B is decreased due to low metabolic activity of the liver.
3. If used in thyrotoxicosis, dose of B.B is increased as metabolic activity of the liver is increased.
4. Do not use propranolol in glaucoma, as it masks pain of glaucoma by anesthetic action. IOP may ↑ without pain which is dangerous as eye may rupture without complain.
5. If used with diuretics to treat salt & water retention (avoid K-sparing diuretics as K+ ↑).

### Q11: Dopamine dose dependant action.
**Model Answer:**
Low dose (1– 5 µg/kg/min):** → D1 receptor stimulation in the kidney, leading to vasodilation of renal arteries and improve renal perfusion.
Moderate dose (5– 10 µg/kg/min):** → β1 stimulation → ↑ heart rate & contractility, improving COP.
High dose (>10 µg/kg/min):** → α1 stimulation → vasoconstriction → ↑ blood pressure.

---

### Q12: Redistribution of drug.
**Model Answer:**
Initial distribution to highly perfused organs (brain, heart, liver).
Then drug redistributes to less perfused tissues (muscle, fat).
Leads to termination of drug effect (e.g., thiopental anesthesia).
Important in lipid-soluble drugs.
