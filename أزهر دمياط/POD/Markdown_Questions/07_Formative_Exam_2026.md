# Formative Exam 2026 Questions (MCQ)

### Q1: Which of the following type of necrosis is found in pyogenic abcess
- **A)** Coagulative necrosis
- **B)** Caseous necrosis
- **C)** Liquefactive necrosis
- **D)** Fat necrosis

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q2: Carbuncles are most commonly associated with which of the following underlying conditions?
- **A)** HIV infection
- **B)** Diabetes mellitus
- **C)** Chronic steroid use
- **D)** Alcoholism

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q3: Homeostasis is best defined as:
- **A)** The process of cell division and growth
- **B)** Cells maintaining normal physiologic function
- **C)** The accumulation of waste products in tissues
- **D)** Rapid adaptation to injury without recovery

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q4: A granuloma is best defined as:
- **A)** Acute suppurative inflammation
- **B)** Chronic non-specific inflammation
- **C)** Serous inflammation
- **D)** Chronic specific inflammation

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q5: The eosinophilic appearance of cells in necrosis is due to:
- **A)** Loss of RNA and protein denaturation
- **B)** Increased nuclear staining
- **C)** Mitochondrial swelling
- **D)** Cytoplasmic vacuolation

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q6: The effect of a competitive antagonist can be overcome by:
- **A)** Increasing antagonist dose
- **B)** Irreversibly binding the receptor
- **C)** Increasing agonist concentration
- **D)** Inhibiting metabolism of antagonist

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q7: β2-adrenoceptors belong to which receptor super-family?
- **A)** Ligand-gated ion channels
- **B)** G-protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs)
- **C)** Receptor tyrosine kinases
- **D)** Nuclear hormone receptors

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q8: For a drug such as piroxicam with a 40-hour half-life and being dosed once daily (i.e., every 24 hours), steady state will be reached shortly following which DOSE (not which half-life)?
- **A)** 1st dose
- **B)** 3rd dose
- **C)** 5th dose
- **D)** 8th dose
- **E)** 12th dose

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q9: X is a drug that is extensively bound to plasma protein, if you give therapeutic dose to a person with severe hypoalbuminemia which one of the following effects would you expect to occur:
- **A)** A greater than normal (possibly toxic) response to the drug
- **B)** A longer duration of action
- **C)** A slower onset of action
- **D)** A drug effect completely different from what normally occur

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q10: With IV infusion, a drug reaches 75% of its final steady state in 6 hours. The elimination half-life of the drug must be about:
- **A)** 3h
- **B)** 12 h
- **C)** 6h
- **D)** 24 h

**Correct Answer:** A
