### Q143: Define: metaplasia, thrombosis, ulcer, resolution, melanoma, and leukocytosis.
**Model Answer:**

### Q144: Compare between metastatic calcification and dystrophic calcification.
**Model Answer:**

### Q145: Compare between benign and malignant tumors.
**Model Answer:**
- Differentiation & Anaplasia: Benign tumors are well-differentiated (closely resemble normal tissue of origin); malignant tumors show poor differentiation or anaplasia.
- Rate of Growth: Benign tumors grow slowly; malignant tumors grow rapidly.
- Local Invasiveness: Benign tumors are circumscribed, cohesive, and usually encapsulated without invading surrounding tissues; malignant tumors are non-encapsulated, locally invasive, and infiltrate surrounding parenchyma.
- Metastasis: Benign tumors NEVER metastasize; malignant tumors have the capacity to spread to distant organs via lymphatics, blood vessels, or transcoelomic seeding.
