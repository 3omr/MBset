### Q1: Factors most likely to contribute to the development of diabetic ketoacidosis include all of the following except

- **A)** Overeating
- **B)** Vomiting
- **C)** Omission of insulin doses
- **D)** Infection

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** marked
**Source Pages:** 1
**EXP:** Omission of insulin doses, infection and dietary excess (overeating) are recognised precipitants of diabetic ketoacidosis; vomiting is a consequence (symptom) of ketoacidosis rather than a primary contributing factor.
