### Q1: About APUD cells, all the following statements are true, except:

- **A)** These are of ectodermal origin arising from the neural crest.
- **B)** Neoplasms of APUD cells may occur simultaneously in different organs.
- **C)** APUD cell neoplasms are always malignant.
- **D)** APUD cells are present in the Islets of Langerhans.
- **E)** APUD cells are present in the adrenal medulla.

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** APUDomas may be benign or malignant; the majority of insulinomas and pheochromocytomas are benign.

### Q2: About multiple endocrine neoplasia (MEN) syndromes, all the following statements are true, except:

### Q3: About the thyroid gland, one statement only is true:

- **A)** Thyroid follicular tissue arises from the third pharyngeal pouch.
- **B)** The parafollicular C cells arise from the floor of the developing pharynx.
- **C)** The thyroglossal duct is normally patent during the first few years of life.
- **D)** Thyroglobulin is stored in colloid that is present inside the thyroid follicles.
- **E)** The parafollicular cells secrete thyroid stimulating hormone.

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Thyroid hormones are stored as thyroglobulin in follicular colloid, while parafollicular C cells secrete calcitonin.

### Q4: About anatomy of the thyroid gland, one statement only is true:

- **A)** The thyroid is enclosed within the prevertebral fascia.
- **B)** The sternomastoid muscles lie anterior to the thyroid.
- **C)** The thyroid gland extends up to the level of the hyoid bone.
- **D)** Lymphatic drainage of the thyroid gland goes primarily to the submandibular lymph nodes.
- **E)** The carotid sheath crosses the anterior surface of the thyroid gland.

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** The source marks B as the single true statement; the gland is enclosed by pretracheal fascia and its lymphatics drain to prelaryngeal, pretracheal, and deep cervical nodes.

### Q5: About anatomical relations of the thyroid gland, all the following statements are true, except:

- **A)** The external laryngeal nerve is related to the superior thyroid vessels.
- **B)** The recurrent laryngeal nerve is related to the inferior thyroid artery.

### Q7: About the physiology of the thyroid gland, all the following statements are true, except:

- **A)** Iodine is necessary for the synthesis of thyroid hormones.
- **B)** The thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH) is secreted by the anterior lobe of the pituitary gland.
- **C)** TSH stimulates the synthesis and release of hormones from the thyroid gland.
- **D)** Total T4 and T3 more accurately reflect the thyroid function status than the free T4 and T3.
- **E)** Calcitonin stimulates the precipitation of calcium in bones.

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Total T4 and T3 are affected by changes in thyroxine-binding proteins; free hormone levels better reflect thyroid function.

### Q11: About management of a clinically simple multinodular goitre, all the following statements are true, except:

### Q13: About retrosternal goitre, all the following statements are true, except:

### Q14: A 17-year-old female presents with a 3 cm thyroid nodule. FNA shows papillary structures. There is no evidence of metastases. The appropriate treatment is:

### Q18: About thyroid cancer, all the following statements are true, except:

### Q26: About primary hyperparathyroidism, one statement only is true:

- **C)** Serum calcitonin is reduced.

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Primary hyperparathyroidism is most commonly due to a parathyroid adenoma; raised PTH mobilizes calcium from bone and causes hypercalcaemia and osteoporosis.

### Q34: A 53-year-old man is accidentally discovered to have a well-defined 2.5 cm right adrenal mass on CT scan done for another reason. Hormonal studies are all normal. The proper management is:

- **B)** Adrenalectomy.

- **D)** Radiotherapy.

### Q37: About Cushing's syndrome, all the following statements are true, except:

- **A)** Cushing's disease is caused by an anterior pituitary adenoma.
- **B)** With adrenal Cushing's syndrome ACTH level is elevated.
- **C)** Truncal obesity is one of the manifestations of the syndrome.

**Answer Source:** marked

### Q38: The risks of adrenalectomy for Cushing's syndrome are increased by all the following, except:

- **A)** Hypertension.
- **B)** Diabetes mellitus.
- **C)** Delayed wound healing.
- **D)** Tendency to gastro-duodenal ulceration.
- **E)** Bronchospasm.

**Correct Answer:** E
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Excess cortisol impairs wound healing and predisposes to gastro-duodenal ulceration; bronchospasm is a feature of carcinoid syndrome, not Cushing's syndrome.
