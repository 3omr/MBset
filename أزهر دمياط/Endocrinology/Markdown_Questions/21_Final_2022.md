### Q1: Incretin-based therapy in the treatment of diabetes mellitus.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Incretin therapy includes GLP-1 receptor agonists and DPP-4 inhibitors. GLP-1 agonists enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppress glucagon, slow gastric emptying and promote satiety and weight loss; DPP-4 inhibitors prolong endogenous incretin activity and are weight neutral.

### Q2: Clinical picture of Addison disease.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Features include weakness, weight loss, anorexia, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, hyperpigmentation, postural hypotension, salt craving, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hypoglycemia and possible adrenal crisis.

### Q3: Indications of insulin.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Indications include type 1 diabetes, diabetic ketoacidosis, hyperosmolar state, pregnancy when needed, severe symptomatic hyperglycemia, and patients with acute illness or inadequate control on non-insulin therapy.

### Q4: Case of hypothyroidism and approach to diagnosis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Confirm with TSH and free T4. Primary hypothyroidism shows high TSH with low free T4; central disease shows low or inappropriately normal TSH with low free T4. Test thyroid peroxidase antibodies when autoimmune disease is suspected, assess complications, and treat with levothyroxine while investigating the cause.

### Q5: Drug causes of hyperprolactinemia.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Important causes include dopamine antagonists such as antipsychotics and metoclopramide, some antidepressants, methyldopa, verapamil, opioids, estrogens and H2 blockers.

---

### Q6: Pituitary causes of hypogonadism.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Causes include pituitary adenoma, prolactinoma, pituitary apoplexy, Sheehan syndrome, hypophysitis, surgery, irradiation, traumatic injury and infiltrative disease.

### Q7: Clinical picture of growth-hormone deficiency.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Children have proportionate short stature, reduced growth velocity, delayed bone age, immature facial appearance, increased truncal fat and sometimes hypoglycemia. Adults may have reduced muscle mass, increased fat, low energy, impaired quality of life and adverse metabolic changes.

---

### Q8: Enumerate functional adrenal neoplasms.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Functional adrenal tumors include cortisol-secreting tumors causing Cushing syndrome, aldosterone-secreting adenomas causing primary hyperaldosteronism, androgen or estrogen-secreting tumors, and catecholamine-secreting pheochromocytomas.

---

### Q9: Causes of panhypopituitarism.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Causes include pituitary or hypothalamic tumors, surgery, radiotherapy, Sheehan syndrome, pituitary apoplexy, traumatic brain injury, hypophysitis, infections such as tuberculosis, and infiltrative disorders such as sarcoidosis or hemochromatosis.
