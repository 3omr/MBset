### Q16: Which of the following is a potential complication of Cushing syndrome?

- **A)** Osteoporosis
- **B)** Hypoglycemia
- **C)** Hypothyroidism
- **D)** Low blood pressure
- **E)** Anemia

**Correct Answer:** A
**Answer Source:** marked
**Source Pages:** 3
**EXP:** Cortisol excess causes osteoporosis (increased bone resorption, reduced osteoblast activity and calcium absorption); Cushing syndrome causes hyperglycaemia, hypertension and polycythaemia rather than hypoglycaemia, low blood pressure or anaemia.
