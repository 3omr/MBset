# Final 2018 Exam Questions

### Q1: One of the following drugs is contraindicated in comatose patient:
- **A)** Oxygen, Narcan (naloxone) 2mg IV push
- **B)** Thiamine 100mg IV push
- **C)** D50 1 AMP IV
- **D)** Syrup of ipecac

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q2: Emesis is contraindicated if:
- **A)** The patient is drowsy, unconscious or having a seizure
- **B)** The ingestion of acetaminophen
- **C)** Ingestion of atropine
- **D)** All of the above

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q3: Indications for endotracheal intubation include:
- **A)** Respiratory arrest, in all unconscious unresponsive to naloxone
- **B)** Impaired protective airway reflexes
- **C)** Coma
- **D)** Status epilepticus

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q4: Indications for mechanical ventilation include:
- **A)** Respiratory arrest (apnea), Severe hypoxemia not responding to high flow rate
- **B)** Hypoventilation and respiratory acidosis
- **C)** Shock
- **D)** Coma

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q5: One of the following is antidote for cyanide:
- **A)** Ca-Na2 EDTA
- **B)** Cyanocobalamin (Hydroxocobalamin)
- **C)** DMSA
- **D)** Penicillamine

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q6: Induction of methemoglobinemia is an important step in the treatment of:
- **A)** CO
- **B)** H2S
- **C)** CO2

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q7: Chelators are the systemic antidotes for metals by:
- **A)** Precipitating the metals in the stomach
- **B)** Counteracting their toxic effects
- **C)** Combine with the metal and easily excreted
- **D)** Oxidation of metal ions to a nontoxic complex

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q8: The following statements are general characters of metals toxicity Except:
- **A)** Their antidotes are chelators
- **B)** All cause GIT irritation and diarrhea
- **C)** All have local and systemic effect
- **D)** Their systemic antidotes are activated charcoal

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q9: Chronic lead poisoning may occur through:
- **A)** Oral route only
- **B)** Dermal route only
- **C)** Inhalation of fumes only
- **D)** Oral route & Dermal route and Inhalation

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q10: In iron poisoning vomiting, bloody diarrhea, massive fluid loss in G.I.T., renal failure and death occurs in:
- **A)** Stage 1
- **B)** Stage 2
- **C)** Stage 3
- **D)** Stage 4

**Correct Answer:** A

### Q11: The first line of management of deep unknown coma is:
- **A)** Immediate gastric lavage & glucose
- **B)** Antidote treatment & glucose

- **D)** Emesis with syrup of ipecac, then glucose
