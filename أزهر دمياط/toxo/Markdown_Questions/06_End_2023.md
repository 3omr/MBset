# End 2023 Exam Questions

### Q1: The quickest & most dangerous route of poison administration is:
- **A)** Intravenous injection followed by intramuscular and intraperitoneal
- **B)** Intravenous injection followed by inhalation and intraperitoneal
- **C)** Intravenous injection followed by intraperitoneal and inhalation
- **D)** Intravenous injection followed by rectum and intraperitoneal

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q2: Opioid toxidrome is characterized by:
- **A)** Miosis
- **B)** Mydriasis
- **C)** Hypertension
- **D)** Diarrhea

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q3: Chelators act as systemic antidotes for heavy metals by:
- **A)** Precipitating the metals in the stomach
- **B)** Counteracting their toxic effects physiologically
- **C)** Competing with the metal and easily excreted
- **D)** Reacting with metal ions to form a stable, soluble, non-toxic complex

**Correct Answer:** D
