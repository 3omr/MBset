### Q2: Which ONE of the following enzyme cleavage sequences would lead to Aβ production from the amyloid precursor protein?
- **A)** α-secretase and then γ-secretase
- **B)** β-secretase and then γ-secretase
- **C)** γ-secretase and then α-secretase
- **D)** γ-secretase and then β-secretase

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The amyloidogenic pathway involves initial cleavage of APP by β-secretase (BACE) followed by γ-secretase, releasing pathogenic Aβ peptides.

### Q11: Which of the following is not a cause of protein misfolding?

### Q15: In the normal (nonamyloidogenic) APP pathway, APP is cleaved by:
- **A)** β-secretase and γ-secretase
- **B)** α-secretase and γ-secretase
- **C)** β-secretase only
- **D)** Caspase enzymes

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** In the non-amyloidogenic pathway, α-secretase cleaves within the Aβ domain, preventing toxic Aβ formation, followed by γ-secretase.

### Q16: In Alzheimer’s disease, amyloid plaques form due to:
- **A)** Accumulation of tau proteins

- **C)** Inflammatory cell infiltration
- **D)** Loss of dopamine

**Correct Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Extracellular amyloid plaques are composed of aggregated, insoluble amyloid-beta (Aβ42) fibrils.
