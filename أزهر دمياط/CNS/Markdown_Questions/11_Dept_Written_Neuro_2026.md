### Q1: Define sensory discrimination and mention two examples of sensory discrimination.
**Model Answer:**
Sensory discrimination is the ability of the nervous system to distinguish between different stimuli. Examples include: 1. Two-point discrimination. 2. Graphesthesia.

### Q2: Write short notes on causes and clinical features of proximal muscle weakness.
**Model Answer:**
Causes: Myasthenia gravis, Myopathies, endocrine and metabolic disorders, drugs. Clinical features: Difficulty rising from a sitting position, difficulty climbing stairs or combing hair; weakness mainly involves shoulder and pelvic girdles; usually symmetrical.

### Q3: What are the red flags in headache that indicate the need for urgent neuroimaging?
**Model Answer:**
1. Sudden "thunderclap" headache (onset within seconds). 2. New or progressive headache in patients over 50 years of age. 3. Headache with focal neurological deficits (motor weakness, speech disturbance, visual loss). 4. Headache with systemic features: fever, neck stiffness, or immunosuppression. 5. Headache following head trauma. 6. Papilledema or altered level of consciousness.
