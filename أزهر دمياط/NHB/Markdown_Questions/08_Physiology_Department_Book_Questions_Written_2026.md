### Question 24

Why is facilitated diffusion saturable at high solute concentrations?

### Question 26

Why does NaCl have greater osmotic activity than glucose?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: NaCl dissociates into two ions (Na+ and Cl-), contributing 2 osmoles per mole, while glucose remains undissociated and contributes only 1 osmole per mole.
