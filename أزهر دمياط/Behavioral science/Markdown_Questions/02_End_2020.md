### Q20: What are sleep regulators?

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
Sleep regulators include:
1. Homeostatic sleep drive (accumulation of adenosine during wakefulness).
2. Circadian rhythm regulated by the Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN) of the hypothalamus.
3. Hormonal regulation: Melatonin secretion by pineal gland in darkness, cortisol in the morning.
4. Neurotransmitters: Serotonin, GABA, orexin/hypocretin, and acetylcholine.
