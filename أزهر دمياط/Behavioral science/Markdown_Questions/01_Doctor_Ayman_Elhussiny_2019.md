### Q1: A 25-year-old male started to use heroin in doses about 1/4 gm daily which gives him intense positive feelings. After a while, this dose became unable to give him the same effect and he started to increase the dose. What is the name of this phenomenon?

- **A)** Craving
- **B)** Tolerance
- **C)** Dependence
- **D)** Compulsion

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q2: What is the neurotransmitter responsible for promoting learning and memory?

- **A)** Glutamate
- **B)** Serotonin
- **C)** GABA
- **D)** Dopamine

**Correct Answer:** A

---

### Q3: The following is true regarding non-rapid eye movement sleep (NREM) EXCEPT:

- **A)** Most of physiological parameters are reduced
- **B)** Includes some fragmented dreams
- **C)** Represents about 20-25 % of total sleep
- **D)** Repair and maintenance of body functions occur during this stage

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q4: The central organs responsible for maintaining consciousness are:

- **A)** The reticular activating system and the limbic system
- **B)** The cerebral cortex and the hypothalamus
- **C)** The reticular activating system and the hypothalamus
- **D)** The reticular activating system and the cerebral cortex

**Correct Answer:** D
