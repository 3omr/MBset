### Q1: A 65-year-old patient presents with progressive memory loss and cognitive decline, diagnosed as Alzheimer's disease. This condition is primarily associated with the degeneration of neurons releasing which neurotransmitter?

- **A)** Dopamine
- **B)** Serotonin
- **C)** Acetylcholine
- **D)** GABA

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q2: A construction worker survives an accident where an iron rod penetrates his skull. He recovers physically but his personality changes drastically; he becomes impulsive, rude, and unable to plan for the future. Which brain lobe was likely damaged?

- **A)** Occipital lobe
- **B)** Parietal lobe
- **C)** Frontal lobe
- **D)** Temporal lobe

**Correct Answer:** C

---

### Q3: A student works hard to get good grades primarily to avoid his parents' criticism and nagging. In terms of operant conditioning, the removal of the nagging acts as:

- **A)** Positive reinforcement
- **B)** Negative reinforcement
- **C)** Positive punishment
- **D)** Negative punishment

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q4: A person is described as sociable, talkative, and seeking external stimulation. According to Eysenck's personality theory, this person scores high on:

- **A)** Neuroticism
- **B)** Psychoticism
- **C)** Introversion
- **D)** Extraversion

**Correct Answer:** D

---

### Q5: A 2-year-old child sees a zebra for the first time and calls it a 'horse'. When his mother points out the stripes and explains the difference, the child modifies his mental concept to distinguish between zebras and horses. This process is called:

- **A)** Assimilation
- **B)** Accommodation
- **C)** Object permanence
- **D)** Conservation

**Correct Answer:** B

---

### Q6: An adult female exhibits unstable interpersonal relationships, a poor self-image, and impulsive behavior. She fears abandonment intensely. This behavior pattern is most strongly associated with which childhood attachment style?

- **A)** Secure attachment
- **B)** Anxious-resistant attachment
- **C)** Anxious-avoidant attachment
- **D)** Disorganized (anxious-disorganized) attachment

**Correct Answer:** D

### Q7: You change your phone number. After a few weeks, you can easily remember the new number, but when someone asks for your old number, you struggle to recall it because the new number blocks it. This is an example of:

- **A)** Proactive interference
- **B)** Retroactive interference
- **C)** Decay
- **D)** Retrograde amnesia

**Correct Answer:** B

### Q8: When solving a diagnosis puzzle, a doctor uses a mental 'rule of thumb' based on typical presentations of diseases, rather than checking every single possible rarity. This cognitive strategy is known as:
