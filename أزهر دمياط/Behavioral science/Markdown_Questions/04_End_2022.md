### Q14: Definition and theories of motivation.

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
Motivation: The internal state that activates, directs, and sustains behavior toward a particular goal.
Theories of motivation:
1. Instinct Theory (biological hardwiring).
2. Drive-Reduction Theory (Hull - physiological needs create drives to restore homeostasis).
3. Arousal Theory (seeking optimal level of arousal).
4. Incentive Theory (external stimuli pull behavior).
5. Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs (physiological, safety, love, esteem, self-actualization).

### Q15: Factors affecting memory.

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
Factors affecting memory include:
1. Attention and concentration (depth of processing).
2. Motivation and emotional state (stress and anxiety impair recall; moderate arousal helps).
3. Rehearsal, repetition, and organization of material (chunking, mnemonics).
4. Sleep and physical health (sleep consolidates memory in the hippocampus).
5. Interference (proactive and retroactive interference).

### Q17: How can you deal with frustration?

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
Ways to deal with frustration include:
1. Problem-solving: Identifying obstacles, analyzing alternatives, and modifying goals.
2. Cognitive restructuring: Reframing negative beliefs and managing expectations.
3. Stress management: Relaxation techniques, deep breathing, and physical exercise.
4. Emotional expression: Constructive communication and seeking social support.
5. Avoiding maladaptive defenses: Preventing aggressive displacement, withdrawal, or substance use.
