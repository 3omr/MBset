**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
1. Consolidation of new declarative (episodic and semantic) memories from short-term to long-term memory.
2. Spatial navigation and cognitive mapping.
3. Contextual fear conditioning and regulation of hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis stress response.

### Q4: Describe the stages of sleep.

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
1. NREM Stage 1: Light transitional sleep, theta waves, reduced heart rate.
2. NREM Stage 2: True sleep, characterized by sleep spindles and K-complexes (~50% of total sleep).
3. NREM Stage 3 & 4 (Slow-Wave Sleep): Deep restorative sleep, delta waves, growth hormone release, physical repair.
4. REM Sleep: Rapid eye movements, low-voltage fast desynchronized EEG, muscle atonia, vivid dreaming, memory consolidation (20-25% of total sleep).

### Q6: Based on theories of motivation, how can you increase motivation of employees in a certain organization?

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
1. Maslow's theory: Ensure basic physiological/safety needs first, then offer recognition, autonomy, and opportunities for self-actualization.
2. Incentive & Reinforcement theory: Implement performance bonuses, variable ratio reinforcement, praise, and constructive feedback.
3. Goal-setting theory: Set specific, measurable, challenging yet attainable goals.
4. Intrinsic motivation: Provide autonomy, skill variety, and purposeful tasks.

### Q7: According to your understanding of psychological aspects of memory, how can students approaching examinations improve their memory?

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
1. Spaced repetition rather than cramming.
2. Active recall and self-testing (testing effect).
3. Elaborative rehearsal: Connecting new facts to existing knowledge, using mnemonics and visualization.
4. Chunking information into manageable units.
5. Ensuring adequate sleep (especially REM/SWS sleep) for hippocampal consolidation.
6. Managing stress and exam anxiety (Yerkes-Dodson law).

### Q8: How can you help people exposed to stressful conditions? List strategies of stress management training and coping skills.

**Type:** QROC

**Model Answer:**
1. Problem-focused coping: Time management, problem-solving skills, assertiveness training.
2. Emotion-focused coping: Cognitive restructuring (reframing catastrophic thoughts), relaxation techniques (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, meditation).
3. Physiological coping: Aerobic exercise, balanced nutrition, adequate sleep.
4. Social support: Counseling, group therapy, peer support networks.
