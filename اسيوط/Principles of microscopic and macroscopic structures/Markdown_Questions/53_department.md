### Question 1

### Question 56

Mention parts and sites of the decidua.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: The decidua is the specialized functional layer of the gravid endometrium shed after parturition. It is divided into three distinct topographic regions:
1. **Decidua basalis**: Situated directly deep to the conceptus at the implantation site, between the blastocyst and myometrium; forms the maternal portion of the placenta.
2. **Decidua capsularis**: Overlies the blastocyst and separates it from the uterine cavity; as pregnancy advances, it stretches, degenerates, and fuses with the decidua parietalis.
3. **Decidua parietalis (decidua vera)**: Lines the remainder of the uterine cavity away from the implantation site.

### Question 57

Describe the morphology and features of the full-term placenta.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: **Morphological Features of the Term Placenta**:
- **Shape**: Discoid, circular to oval plate.
- **Dimensions**: Diameter 15-20 cm, central thickness 2-3 cm.
- **Weight**: 500-600 grams (approximately 1/6 of normal fetal birth weight).
- **Fetal Surface**: Smooth, shiny, covered completely by transparent amnion; the umbilical cord inserts near its center, with branching umbilical chorionic vessels radiating outward beneath the amnion.
- **Maternal Surface**: Dull, dark brownish-red, divided by deep decidual grooves/septa into 15-20 convex lobes called cotyledons; covered by a thin layer of decidua basalis with remnants of torn spiral arterioles.

### Question 58

List the layers of the placental barrier (membrane) across pregnancy.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: **Placental Barrier Layers**:
1. **Early Pregnancy (First Trimester - 4 continuous layers)**:
   - Syncytiotrophoblast (outer layer).
   - Cytotrophoblast (layer of Langhans).
   - Extraembryonic mesoderm (connective tissue core of the tertiary villus).
   - Endothelium of fetal intravillous capillaries.
2. **Late Pregnancy / Term (Thinned barrier - 2 main functional layers)**:
   - Syncytiotrophoblast (cytotrophoblast largely disappears, forming vasculosyncytial membranes).
   - Endothelium of fetal capillaries (resting on a fused basement membrane with syncytium) to facilitate rapid gas and nutrient diffusion.

---

### Question 59

List four congenital structural anomalies of the placenta.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: **Placental Anomalies (Four Examples)**:
1. **Placenta Previa**: Implantation of the placenta in the lower uterine segment, partially or completely covering the internal cervical os.
2. **Placenta Accreta**: Abnormal adherence of chorionic villi directly to the myometrium due to absence or deficiency of decidua basalis.
3. **Battledore Placenta (Marginal Insertion)**: The umbilical cord attaches at the placental margin rather than centrally.
4. **Velamentous Insertion of the Cord**: Umbilical vessels separate and travel through the fetal membranes before reaching the placental rim.
5. **Placenta Succenturiata**: One or more small accessory lobes developed at a distance from the main placenta and connected by vascular bridges.

---

### Question 60

Enumerate the cellular and tissue layers of the chorion.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: The chorionic wall is composed of three continuous layers:
1. **Syncytiotrophoblast**: The outer multinucleated syncytial layer without distinct cell boundaries, in direct contact with maternal blood in intervillous spaces.
2. **Cytotrophoblast (Cells of Langhans)**: The inner layer of mononucleated, cuboidal trophoblastic stem cells.
3. **Somatopleuric Extraembryonic Mesoderm**: The internal layer of embryonic connective tissue lining the cytotrophoblast and forming the mesenchymal stroma of the chorionic plate.
