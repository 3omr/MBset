### Question 1

Enumerate the layers forming the placental barrier in the early stages of pregnancy.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Endothelial lining of fetal vessels, 2. Connective tissue of the villous core, 3. Cytotrophoblastic layer, 4. Syncytiotrophoblast.

### Question 2

Mention 3 functions of the yolk sac.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Nutritive function in the early stages of development, 2. Contributes some of the first blood cells, 3. Provides germ cells which migrate to the gonads to form eggs and sperm, 4. Part of it forms the basis of the gut.

### Question 3

Mona is a 32 years old woman complaining of infertility. She goes to the hospital for medical advice. The doctor prescribes drugs which stimulate ovulation. Ultrasound examination revealed the presence of follicles in different stages of maturation in her ovaries, including a mature Graafian follicle. What is the definition of oogenesis, and describe the structure of a mature Graafian follicle.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: It is the process of transformation of oogonia into mature ovum. Structure of mature Graafian follicle: 1. The follicular cavity (antrum) is large and filled with follicular fluid. 2. The ovum is pushed to one side and surrounded by the zona pellucida and granulosa cells (cumulus oophorus). 3. The follicle wall consists of membrana granulosa, theca interna, and theca externa. 4. Diameter is about 25 mm or more.

### Question 4

Define neurulation. Mention 2 derivatives of the ectodermal germ layer.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: It is the process of formation of the neural tube. Derivatives: Brain, spinal cord, epidermis, hair, nails, cutaneous glands (sweat, sebaceous, mammary), parotid gland, pituitary gland, neural crest derivatives.

### Question 5

Enumerate 3 external features of a normal full term fetus at the time of birth.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Skin is covered by vernix caseosa. 2. Skull has the largest circumference of all parts of the body. 3. Weight is 3,000 to 3,400 g. 4. CRL is about 36 cm and CHL is about 50 cm. 5. Sexual characteristics are pronounced and testes are in the scrotum.

### Question 6

A 25 years old pregnant female in the 9th month of pregnancy presents for follow up. During ultrasound examination, the doctor observes that the umbilical cord encircles the neck of the fetus. Which anomaly of the umbilical cord could cause this abnormality, and mention the contents of the umbilical cord in a full term fetus?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Anomaly: Abnormally long cord. Contents: Umbilical vessels (2 umbilical arteries and 1 umbilical vein) surrounded by Wharton's jelly.
