### Question 1

During an operation, the surgeon found an abdominal mass. A biopsy was taken for immediate microscopic examination before completing the surgery. Which technique is used to prepare the biopsy rapidly?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Freezing technique (using a Cryostat / liquid nitrogen) produces rapid sections within minutes for immediate intraoperative diagnosis.

---

### Question 2

What instrument is used to cut frozen sections in the freezing technique?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Cryostat (a microtome enclosed inside a refrigerated chamber).

---

### Question 3

What is the main fixative routinely used in paraffin section preparation?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 10% Neutral Buffered Formalin (formol saline).

### Question 4

What is the most widely used routine histological staining system?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Hematoxylin and Eosin (H&E) staining system.

### Question 5

Hematoxylin is a basic dye that stains acidic cell components (such as DNA/RNA in the nucleus) what color?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Blue / purple (basophilic).

---

### Question 6

Eosin is an acidic dye that stains basic cytoplasmic proteins what color?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Pink / red (acidophilic / eosinophilic).

### Question 7

In a cell, the synthesis of the motor protein kinesin was artificially blocked. Which process will be primarily damaged?

- **A)** Retrograde axonal transport
- **B)** Anterograde transport from cell center to periphery
- **C)** Ribosome assembly
- **D)** DNA replication

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Kinesin is an ATP-dependent motor protein that moves cargo along microtubules toward the plus (+) end (anterograde transport from nucleus/cell center toward periphery).

---

### Question 8

Which cellular organelle is responsible for the generation of ATP via oxidative phosphorylation?

- **A)** Lysosome
- **B)** Mitochondria
- **C)** Golgi apparatus
- **D)** Nucleolus

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Mitochondria generate ATP through the citric acid cycle and oxidative phosphorylation.

---

### Question 9

Fast axonal and intracellular organellar transport occurs along which cytoskeletal element?

- **A)** Intermediate filaments
- **B)** Microfilaments
- **C)** Microtubules
- **D)** Thick filaments

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Microtubules serve as intracellular tracks for bidirectional organelle and vesicle transport powered by dynein and kinesin.

---

### Question 10

Which motor protein moves vesicles in the retrograde direction along microtubules (toward the minus end)?

- **A)** Kinesin
- **B)** Dynein
- **C)** Actin
- **D)** Myosin

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Cytoplasmic dynein is the motor protein responsible for retrograde transport toward the minus end of microtubules.
