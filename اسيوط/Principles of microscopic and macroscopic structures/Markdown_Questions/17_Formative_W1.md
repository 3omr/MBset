### Question 1

Lysosomes originate from:

- **A)** Microbodies
- **B)** Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
- **C)** Nucleus
- **D)** Golgi complex
- **E)** Mitochondria

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Primary lysosomes bud off from the trans-Golgi network.

### Question 2

Which of the following is not a variety of the deep fascia:

- **A)** Capsule of the kidney
- **B)** Intermuscular septa
- **C)** Retinacula
- **D)** Camper's fascia

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Camper's fascia is the superficial fatty layer of the superficial fascia of the anterior abdominal wall.

### Question 3

The function of the centrosome is:

- **A)** Packing of secretion
- **B)** Disposal of damaged cell components
- **C)** ATP production
- **D)** Formation of mitotic spindle
- **E)** Protein synthesis

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The centrosome (containing centrioles) acts as the main microtubule-organizing center (MTOC) that forms the mitotic spindle during cell division.

### Question 4

Which form of muscles has two rows of muscle fibers, facing in opposite diagonal directions, with a central tendon, like a feather:

- **A)** Bipennate
- **B)** Triangular
- **C)** Parallel
- **D)** Fusiform

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Bipennate muscles (e.g. rectus femoris) have muscle fibers inserting obliquely into both sides of a central tendon like the barbs of a feather.

### Question 5

Which of the following is bound to one of the two surfaces of plasma membrane, particularly on the cytoplasmic side:

- **A)** Transmembrane protein
- **B)** Cell coat
- **C)** Carbohydrates
- **D)** Lipid bilayer
- **E)** Peripheral protein

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Peripheral proteins do not penetrate the lipid bilayer; they bind loosely to the hydrophilic heads or to integral proteins, predominantly on the inner cytoplasmic face.

### Question 6

Regarding long bones, choose the wrong statement:

- **A)** During growth they structurally show different regions like diaphysis, metaphysis and epiphysis
- **B)** They receive blood supply from one or more nutrient arteries, periosteal arteries and periarticular arteries
- **C)** They provide levers for movement
- **D)** They are ossified in membrane

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Long bones develop through endochondral (cartilaginous) ossification, not intramembranous ossification.

### Question 7

... is any structure nearer to the back of the body:

- **A)** Distal
- **B)** Anterior
- **C)** Proximal
- **D)** Posterior

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Posterior (dorsal) describes a structure positioned toward or nearer the back of the body.

### Question 8

Which of the following specifically stains glycogen granules for light microscopic examination:

- **A)** Perls Prussian blue reaction
- **B)** PAS reaction
- **C)** Von Kossa stain
- **D)** Osmic acid

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Periodic acid-Schiff (PAS) reaction stains glycogen and other carbohydrates magenta/purple-red.

### Question 9

You have a biopsy of lipid-rich tissue. Which of the following is best used to study this biopsy:

- **A)** Periodic acid-Schiff reaction
- **B)** Paraffin sections
- **C)** Frozen sections
- **D)** Unstained tissue
- **E)** Orcein stain

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Routine paraffin preparation involves organic clearing solvents (xylene) that dissolve lipids; frozen sections stained with fat-soluble dyes (e.g. Sudan III or Oil Red O) preserve lipid droplets.

---

### Question 10

The Golgi complex is formed of:

- **A)** Vesicles only
- **B)** Tubules of smooth membranes
- **C)** Filamentous structure
- **D)** Cisternae and vesicles
- **E)** Cisternae of smooth membrane only

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The Golgi apparatus is composed of stacks of flattened membrane-bound cisternae and associated transport and secretory vesicles.
