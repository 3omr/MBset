### Question 31

Enumerate the anatomical coverings (fascial capsules) of the kidney from within outwards.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Fibrous capsule (true capsule): Closely applied to renal cortex.
2. Perirenal (perinephric) fat: Adipose capsule surrounding the fibrous capsule.
3. Renal fascia (fascia of Gerota): Fibroelastic sheath enclosing kidney and adrenal gland.
4. Pararenal (paranephric) fat: Retroperitoneal fat situated posterior and external to renal fascia.

---

### Question 32

List the three commonest anatomical sites of ureteric stone impaction (ureteric constrictions).

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Pelviureteric junction (PUJ): Where the renal pelvis joins the upper ureter.
2. Pelvic brim: Where the ureter crosses anterior to the bifurcation of the common iliac (or external iliac) vessels.
3. Ureterovesical junction (UVJ): Where the ureter passes obliquely through the muscular wall of the urinary bladder (narrowest site).

### Question 33

Enumerate the four anatomical parts of the male urethra and state their approximate lengths.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Pre-prostatic (intramural) urethra: ~1 cm, within bladder neck.
2. Prostatic urethra: ~3-4 cm, traversing prostate gland (widest part).
3. Membranous urethra: ~1-2 cm, traversing deep perineal pouch / urogenital diaphragm (narrowest part).
4. Spongy (penile) urethra: ~15 cm, traversing corpus spongiosum of penis (longest part; total length ~20 cm).

---

### Question 34

Enumerate the anatomical subdivisions of the uterus.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Fundus: Dome-shaped part lying above the entrance of the uterine tubes.
2. Body: Main triangular part between fundus and isthmus.
3. Isthmus: Constricted lower segment (~1 cm) between body and cervix.
4. Cervix: Narrow inferior cylinder divided into supravaginal part and vaginal part (portio vaginalis).

---

### Question 35

Describe the normal anatomical position of the human uterus, defining anteversion and anteflexion.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: The normal position is Anteverted and Anteflexed:
1. Anteversion: The long axis of the cervix is bent forward on the long axis of the vagina at an angle of approximately 90 degrees.
2. Anteflexion: The long axis of the uterine body is bent forward on the long axis of the cervix at an angle of approximately 120 to 170 degrees over the superior surface of the empty urinary bladder.
