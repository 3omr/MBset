### Question 1

A 22-year-old male was diagnosed with thalassemia and splenomegaly. Examination of a splenic biopsy shows iron-laden macrophages. Regarding macrophages, which statement is NOT correct?

- **A)** It is one of the fixed connective tissue cells
- **B)** The organelle that is numerous is the lysosome
- **C)** It can be identified with vital stains such as Trypan blue
- **D)** It is differentiated from circulating blood monocytes

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Macrophages are wandering (transient/free) cells of connective tissue belonging to the mononuclear phagocyte system, not permanent fixed structural cells.

### Question 2

What are the resident macrophages in the liver sinusoids called?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Kupffer cells.

### Question 3

What is the main physiological function of macrophages?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Phagocytosis of cellular debris, pathogens, and foreign material, and antigen presentation to T-lymphocytes.

### Question 4

Monocytes leave blood vessels to enter connective tissue by a process called:

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Diapedesis (transendothelial migration / extravasation).

### Question 5

A 26-year-old teacher with asthma experiences bronchoconstriction triggered by mast cell degranulation. A mast cell is:

- **A)** A free connective tissue cell
- **B)** One of the white blood cells
- **C)** Derived from B-lymphocytes
- **D)** A multinucleated giant cell

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Mast cells are free (migratory) connective tissue cells containing cytoplasmic metachromatic granules.

---

### Question 6

Mast cell secretory granules display metachromasia (staining purple-red with blue dyes like toluidine blue) because of high content of:

- **A)** Acidophilic proteins
- **B)** Orcein-positive fibers
- **C)** Metachromatic polyanionic proteoglycans (heparin)
- **D)** Neutral lipids

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The high density of sulfate groups in heparin gives mast cell granules their characteristic metachromatic properties.

### Question 7

Which leukocyte in peripheral blood smear is primarily responsible for elimination of parasitic infections and modulating allergic responses?

- **A)** Antibody production
- **B)** Elimination of parasites
- **C)** Collagen biosynthesis
- **D)** Histamine release
- **E)** Phagocytosis of bacteria

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Eosinophils contain major basic protein and eosinophil cationic protein, specialized in eliminating helminthic parasites.

### Question 8

What is the average normal diameter (in microns) of erythrocytes visible in human peripheral blood smears?

- **A)** 2.0
- **B)** 4.5
- **C)** 5.4
- **D)** 7.6
- **E)** 10.0

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The average diameter of human red blood cells is approximately 7.5 to 7.8 microns (used as an internal histological ruler ~7.6 um).
