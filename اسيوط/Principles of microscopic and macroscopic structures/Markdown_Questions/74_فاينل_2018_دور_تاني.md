### Question 1

Define fertilization, name its normal anatomical site, and enumerate the results of fertilization.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: Fusion of the spermatozoon with the mature ovum to form a single-celled zygote. Site: Lateral third (ampulla) of the uterine tube. Results: 1. Restoration of the diploid number of chromosomes. 2. Determination of the sex of the embryo. 3. Initiation of cleavage.

---

### Question 2

Describe the menstrual (uterine) cycle regarding definition and phases.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: Cyclic changes occurring in the endometrium of the uterus in response to ovarian hormones during the fertile years. Phases: 1. Menstrual phase (days 1-4). 2. Proliferative (follicular) phase (days 5-14). 3. Secretory (luteal) phase (days 15-28).

---

### Question 3

Mention briefly the differentiation of embryonic somites.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Somites differentiate into: 1. Sclerotome: migrates medially to form vertebrae and ribs. 2. Myotome: forms skeletal musculature of trunk and limbs. 3. Dermatome: forms the dermis of the skin.

---

### Question 4

Identify the embryological origin of neural crest cells and name 4 of its derivatives.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Origin: Dorsal margins of the neural folds (neuroectoderm). Derivatives: 1. Dorsal root spinal ganglia and sensory cranial ganglia. 2. Autonomic (sympathetic and parasympathetic) ganglia. 3. Schwann cells and satellite cells. 4. Melanocytes of the skin. 5. Adrenal medulla chromaffin cells. 6. Head mesenchyme and branchial arch skeleton.

### Question 6

Write a short note on types of chorionic villi according to histological structure.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Primary villi: core of cytotrophoblast covered by an outer layer of syncytiotrophoblast. 2. Secondary villi: core of extraembryonic mesoderm surrounded by cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast. 3. Tertiary villi: extraembryonic mesodermal core containing fetal capillaries, covered by cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast.

### Question 8

Define a fibrous joint and mention 3 of its types, with an example for each.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: A joint in which bones are united directly by dense fibrous connective tissue with little or no movement. Types: 1. Sutures (e.g., skull sutures). 2. Syndesmosis (e.g., inferior tibiofibular joint, interosseous membranes). 3. Gomphosis (e.g., dentoalveolar joint / teeth in sockets).

### Question 17

Enumerate 4 characteristic histological features of epithelium.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. High cellularity with closely aggregated cells and minimal intercellular substance. 2. Rests upon a basal lamina (basement membrane). 3.
