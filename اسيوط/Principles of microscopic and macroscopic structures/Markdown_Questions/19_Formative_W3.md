### Question 1

Chromosomal pattern of Down syndrome is:

- **A)** 45+XY, +21
- **B)** 45+X0
- **C)** 44+XXY
- **D)** 47+XYY

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Down syndrome (Trisomy 21) is characterized by an extra chromosome 21 (47 chromosomes total: 45 autosomes + XY or 47,XY,+21).

### Question 2

Regarding stem cells, which of the following is correct?

- **A)** They have reduced potentiality.
- **B)** They are large in number.
- **C)** They divide asymmetrically.
- **D)** They have no plasticity.
- **E)** They divide frequently.

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Stem cells undergo asymmetric cell division, producing one stem cell and one progenitor cell committed to differentiation.

### Question 3

Regarding the epididymis, which is true?

- **A)** It regulates the testicular temperature.
- **B)** It has no cavity.
- **C)** It produces testosterone hormone.
- **D)** It is responsible for sperm maturation.
- **E)** It is present in the abdominal cavity.

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The epididymis is the site of sperm maturation and storage, where sperms acquire motility and fertilizing capacity.

### Question 4

Regarding the left atrium, choose the wrong statement:

- **A)** It receives the openings of the four pulmonary veins.
- **B)** The tricuspid valve connects the left atrium with the left ventricle.
- **C)** It forms the greater part of the base of the heart.
- **D)** Its cavity has a smooth part and rough part.

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The bicuspid (mitral) valve connects the left atrium with the left ventricle; the tricuspid valve connects the right atrium with the right ventricle.

### Question 5

Spermatogenesis starts to occur:

- **A)** After puberty.
- **B)** During intrauterine life.
- **C)** Early in childhood.
- **D)** After marriage.

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Spermatogenesis begins at puberty under the influence of rising gonadotropin levels (LH and FSH).

### Question 6

The amount of DNA in the cell at G2 phase is:

- **A)** Double that at G1 phase.
- **B)** Double that at S phase.
- **C)** Half that at G0 phase.
- **D)** Similar to that at G1 phase.
- **E)** Similar to that at G0 phase.

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: During S phase, DNA replicates; therefore, in G2 phase the DNA content (4C) is double that of G1 phase (2C).

### Question 7

The average viable period of spermatozoa is about:

- **A)** One year.
- **B)** 120 days.
- **C)** 3-4 days.
- **D)** One week.
- **E)** 64 days.

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The average survival / fertilizing lifespan of spermatozoa in the female genital tract is approximately 48 to 72 hours (3-4 days).

### Question 8

The medulla oblongata is connected to the cerebellum by:

- **A)** Crus cerebri.
- **B)** Vermis.
- **C)** Middle cerebellar peduncle.
- **D)** Inferior cerebellar peduncle.
- **E)** Superior cerebellar peduncle.

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The inferior cerebellar peduncle connects the medulla oblongata to the cerebellum.

### Question 9

The oocyte finishes its second meiotic division:

- **A)** After entry of the spermatozoa.
- **B)** Immediately after ovulation.
- **C)** Prenatally.
- **D)** Immediately before ovulation.
- **E)** Before puberty.

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Secondary oocyte is arrested in metaphase II until fertilization occurs (triggered by entry of sperm).

### Question 10

The sympathetic part of the autonomic nervous system is connected centrally to:

- **A)** Sacral and coccygeal segments.
- **B)** Thoracic and sacral segments.
- **C)** Cervical and thoracic segments.
- **D)** Thoracic and lumbar segments.

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: The sympathetic outflow is thoracolumbar, originating from the lateral horn cells of T1 to L2/L3 spinal cord segments.

### Question 11

Which of the following is a characteristic feature of the right lung?

- **A)** It is short and wide with 3 lobes.
- **B)** Its anterior border has the lingula and cardiac notch.
- **C)** It is formed of 2 lobes.
- **D)** It is long and narrow.
- **E)** It has one fissure.

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: The right lung is shorter and broader than the left lung (due to the liver below) and is divided into three lobes (superior, middle, inferior) by oblique and horizontal fissures.

### Question 12

Which of the following statements concerning the ureter is incorrect?

- **A)** It has sites of constrictions along its course.
- **B)** It is a continuation of the renal pelvis.
- **C)** It runs behind the peritoneum.
- **D)** It transports urine from kidney to urinary bladder.
- **E)** It is about 10-15 cm in length.

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: The human ureter is approximately 25-30 cm in length, not 10-15 cm.
