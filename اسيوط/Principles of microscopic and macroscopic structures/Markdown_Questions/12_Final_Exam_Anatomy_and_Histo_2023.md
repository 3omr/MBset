### Question 1

Define spermiogenesis and mention the changes which occur during this process.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: It is the process by which the spermatid is transformed into spermatozoon. Changes: 1. The centriole divides into two parts: axial filament grows from one while the other forms the annulus. 2. The nucleus forms the head of the spermatozoon and the Golgi bodies form the acrosomal cap. 3. Part of the axial filament becomes surrounded by mitochondria forming the mitochondrial sheath. 4. Most of the cytoplasm is extruded and the cell membrane remains as a covering. 5. Axial filament elongates to form the tail.

### Question 30

What is the function of gap junctions in cardiac muscle cells?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: In cardiac muscle cells small ions move rapidly through gap junctions, providing electrical coupling of cardiac muscle cells, permitting synchronous contraction.

---



### Question 31

Compare between exocytosis and endocytosis.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Exocytosis: Means bulk movement of macromolecules out of the cell by fusion of secretory vesicles with the plasma membrane. Endocytosis: Means bulk movement of macromolecules into the cell by formation of vesicles at the plasma membrane.

---
