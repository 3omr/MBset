### Question 1

Give a short note about the structure of the synovial joint.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Structure consists of: 1. Articular bones, 2. Articular cartilage covering bony ends, 3. Fibrous capsule surrounding the articular bony parts, 4. Synovial membrane lining the inner surface of capsule and secreting synovial fluid, 5. Synovial fluid lubricating articular surfaces, 6. Ligaments (extracapsular or intracapsular).

---

### Question 2

Mention the types of chorionic villi and describe the structure of each.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Primary villi: core of cytotrophoblast covered by syncytiotrophoblast. 2. Secondary villi: core of extraembryonic mesoderm covered by cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast. 3. Tertiary villi: extraembryonic mesoderm core containing fetal blood capillaries, covered by cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast.

---

### Question 3

Enumerate the results of fertilization.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Restoration of the diploid number of chromosomes. 2. Determination of the sex of the embryo. 3. Initiation of cleavage.

---

### Question 4

Mention 3 functions of the yolk sac.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Nutritive function in the early stages of development. 2. Contributes some of the first blood cells. 3. Provides primordial germ cells which migrate to the gonads. 4. Incorporated into the embryonic gut.

---

### Question 5

**Correct Answer**: -

### Question 6

Mention 4 endodermal derivatives.

**Correct Answer**: -
