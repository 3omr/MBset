### Question 1
Mention the layers of the basement membrane.
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: The basement membrane consists of:
1. Basal lamina: produced by epithelial cells, composed of lamina lucida and lamina densa (type IV collagen, laminin, proteoglycans).
2. Reticular lamina: produced by connective tissue fibroblasts, composed of type III collagen (reticular fibers) and fibronectin.

3. Induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs).

### Question 3
Enumerate the ultrastructural constituents of the Golgi complex.
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Flattened cisternae / saccules (curved parallel membrane-bounded sacs with cis and trans faces).

3. Condensing vacuoles / secretory vesicles (bud off from trans face for secretion or lysosome formation).

### Question 44
What are the changes in the endometrium after decidual reaction?
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Under progesterone influence:
1. Endometrial stromal cells enlarge, round up, and become lipid- and glycogen-rich (decidual cells).
2. Endometrial glands become highly tortuous, dilated, and hypersecretory.
3. Vascularity increases with dilation and engorgement of spiral arteries.
4. Division into decidua basalis, decidua capsularis, and decidua parietalis.

### Question 45
Mention three differences between cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast.
**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Cellular structure: Cytotrophoblast consists of discrete mononucleated cuboidal cells with distinct cell borders; Syncytiotrophoblast is a multinucleated protoplasmic mass without intercellular boundaries.
2. Mitotic activity: Cytotrophoblast undergoes active mitotic division and provides cells that fuse into the syncytium; Syncytiotrophoblast has no mitotic activity.
3. Endocrine and invasive function: Syncytiotrophoblast produces hCG and secretes proteolytic enzymes to erode maternal endometrium; Cytotrophoblast does not directly secrete hormones.
