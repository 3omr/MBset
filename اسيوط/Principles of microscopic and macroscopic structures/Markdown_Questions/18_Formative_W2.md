### Question 1

Which of the following is the medial bone of the leg?

- **A)** Femur
- **B)** Tibia
- **C)** Ulna
- **D)** Fibula

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The tibia is the large, weight-bearing, medial bone of the leg; the fibula is lateral.

---

### Question 2

Regarding stem cells, which of the following is correct?

- **A)** They have reduced potentiality
- **B)** They divide frequently
- **C)** They are large in number
- **D)** They divide asymmetrically
- **E)** They have no plasticity

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Stem cells undergo asymmetric division: one daughter cell remains a self-renewing stem cell, while the other enters a differentiation pathway.

### Question 3

Regarding the anatomy of the trachea, choose the WRONG statement:

- **A)** The trachea is about 12 cm long
- **B)** It begins at the lower border of thyroid cartilage
- **C)** It extends from level of 6th cervical vertebra to the upper border of the 5th thoracic vertebra
- **D)** It divides into right and left bronchi at the level of sternal angle

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The trachea begins at the lower border of the cricoid cartilage (level of C6), not the thyroid cartilage.

### Question 4

Which of the following specifically stains glycogen granules for light microscopic examination?

- **A)** Osmic acid
- **B)** PAS reaction
- **C)** Von Kossa stain
- **D)** Perls Prussian blue reaction
- **E)** Hematoxylin and Eosin

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Periodic acid-Schiff (PAS) reaction specifically demonstrates glycogen, basement membranes, and mucin.

### Question 5

The amount of DNA in the cell at G2 phase is:

- **A)** Similar to that at G1 phase
- **B)** Similar to that at G0 phase
- **C)** Half that at G0 phase
- **D)** Double that at G1 phase
- **E)** Double that at S phase

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: DNA replication occurs during S phase; thus cells in G2 have duplicated their DNA (4n content), which is double the diploid (2n) content of G1 phase.

### Question 6

Regarding the anatomy of the vertebral column, choose the WRONG statement:

- **A)** The thoracic vertebrae are 12 in number
- **B)** It extends from the base of the skull to the sacrum
- **C)** The cervical vertebrae are 7 in number
- **D)** Each vertebra has 7 processes arising from its different parts

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The vertebral column extends from the base of the skull to the tip of the coccyx, not the sacrum.

---

### Question 7

Karyotyping needs which of the following?

- **A)** Non-dividing cells
- **B)** Adipose tissue sample
- **C)** Colchicine
- **D)** Hematoxylin and Eosin
- **E)** Orcein stain

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Colchicine arrests actively dividing cells at metaphase by inhibiting mitotic spindle formation, allowing chromosomes to be visualized and photographed for karyotyping.

### Question 8

Regarding the left lung, choose the WRONG statement:

- **A)** Longer than the right lung
- **B)** Has a cardiac notch
- **C)** Has three lobes
- **D)** Has lingula

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The left lung has only two lobes (superior and inferior) divided by an oblique fissure; the right lung has three lobes.

### Question 9

The scapula is articulated with the humerus to form:

- **A)** Acromioclavicular joint
- **B)** Shoulder joint
- **C)** Sternoclavicular joint
- **D)** Elbow joint

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The glenoid cavity of the scapula articulates with the head of the humerus to form the glenohumeral (shoulder) joint.

### Question 10

Nucleoplasm is continuous with the cytoplasm through which of the following?

- **A)** Endoplasmic reticulum
- **B)** Nuclear chromatin
- **C)** Centromere
- **D)** Nuclear pores
- **E)** Nuclear lamins

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Nuclear pore complexes span the nuclear envelope and provide aqueous channels for nucleocytoplasmic transport.
