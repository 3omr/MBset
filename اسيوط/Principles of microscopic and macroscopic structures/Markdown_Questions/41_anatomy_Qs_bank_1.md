### Question 29

Mention three anatomical differences between the right and left lungs regarding size/weight, lobes/fissures, and cardiac notch.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Size & length: Right lung is shorter, broader, heavier, and has larger capacity (due to high right dome of diaphragm); Left lung is longer, narrower, and lighter.
2. Lobes & fissures: Right lung has 3 lobes (superior, middle, inferior) divided by 2 fissures (oblique and horizontal); Left lung has 2 lobes (superior and inferior) divided by 1 fissure (oblique).
3. Cardiac notch: Right lung has no cardiac notch on its anterior border; Left lung has a prominent cardiac notch and a projection called the lingula.
