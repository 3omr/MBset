### Question 1

Which of the following is correct about mammalian testes?

- **A)** Graafian follicles, Sertoli cells, Leydig cells
- **B)** Graafian follicles, Sertoli cells, Seminiferous tubules
- **C)** Sertoli cells, Seminiferous tubules, Leydig cells
- **D)** Graafian follicle, Leydig cells, Seminiferous tubule

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Testes contain seminiferous tubules lined by Sertoli cells and germ cells, with Leydig cells in the interstitial space. Graafian follicles are ovarian structures.

### Question 2

Temperature of the scrotum which is necessary for the functioning of testis is always around:

- **A)** 2°C below body temperature
- **B)** 4°C below body temperature
- **C)** 6°C below body temperature
- **D)** 8°C below body temperature

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Normal spermatogenesis requires a scrotal temperature approximately 2°C to 3°C below core body temperature.

### Question 3

The nutritive cells found in seminiferous tubules are:

- **A)** Leydig cells
- **B)** Atretic follicular cells
- **C)** Sertoli cells
- **D)** Chromaffin cells

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Sertoli cells (sustentacular cells) provide structural and nutritional support to developing germ cells throughout spermatogenesis.

### Question 4

Sertoli cells are regulated by the pituitary hormone known as:

- **A)** LH
- **B)** FSH
- **C)** GH
- **D)** Prolactin

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) acts directly on Sertoli cells to stimulate spermatogenesis and androgen-binding protein production.

### Question 5

In spermatogenesis, reduction division of chromosomes occurs during conversion of:

- **A)** Spermatogonia to primary spermatocytes
- **B)** Primary spermatocytes to secondary spermatocytes
- **C)** Secondary spermatocytes to spermatids
- **D)** Spermatids to sperms

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Meiosis I (reduction division) converts diploid primary spermatocytes (46 chromosomes, 4N DNA) into haploid secondary spermatocytes (23 chromosomes, 2N DNA).

### Question 6

Which of the following groups of cells in the male gonad represent haploid cells?

- **A)** Spermatogonial cells
- **B)** Germinal epithelial cells
- **C)** Secondary spermatocytes
- **D)** Primary spermatocytes

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Secondary spermatocytes, spermatids, and spermatozoa are haploid (23 chromosomes).

### Question 7

A single ejaculate contains approximately how many sperms?

- **A)** 20 - 30 thousands
- **B)** 200 - 300 thousands
- **C)** 20 - 30 million
- **D)** 200 - 300 million
- **E)** 500 million at least

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: A normal ejaculate contains about 200 to 300 million spermatozoa (around 50-100 million per mL in a 2-5 mL volume).

### Question 8

Which of the following parts of sperm is responsible for production of energy?

- **A)** Mitochondrial sheath
- **B)** Nucleus
- **C)** Acrosomal cap
- **D)** Tail

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: The mitochondrial sheath in the middle piece of the sperm produces ATP for motility via oxidative phosphorylation.

### Question 9

A 28-year-old lady and a 32-year-old man were trying to conceive for the past 4 years with unexplained subfertility. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a mature sperm cell?

- **A)** An acrosome containing digestive enzymes
- **B)** A large amount of cytoplasm surrounding the nucleus
- **C)** A midpiece and a long tail called a flagellum
- **D)** A mitochondrial sheath around middle piece
- **E)** A nucleus with 22 autosomes and 1 sex chromosome

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: During spermiogenesis, almost all cytoplasm is shed as residual bodies; a mature sperm has virtually no surrounding cytoplasm.

### Question 10

Sperm cells must undergo which process within the female reproductive tract before they can fertilize a secondary oocyte?

- **A)** Capacitation
- **B)** Meiosis
- **C)** Spermiogenesis
- **D)** None of the above

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Capacitation occurs within the female reproductive tract, conditioning the spermatozoa to undergo the acrosome reaction and fertilize the oocyte.

### Question 11

Sperms develop in the:

- **A)** Prostate gland
- **B)** Bulbourethral glands
- **C)** Seminiferous tubules
- **D)** Seminal vesicles
- **E)** Hypothalamus

**Correct Answer**: C

### Question 12

Fusion of two haploid sex cells to produce a diploid zygote is:

- **A)** Capacitation
- **B)** Fertilization
- **C)** Development
- **D)** Differentiation

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Fertilization is the process by which male and female haploid gametes fuse to form a single diploid zygote.

### Question 13

All of a woman's primary oocytes are produced:

- **A)** Between the ages of 16 and 24 years
- **B)** Within a year after she reaches puberty
- **C)** During fetal life before birth
- **D)** None of the above

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Oogenesis begins in prenatal life; all primary oocytes are formed by the 5th month of intrauterine development.

### Question 14

Which of the following statements about oogenesis in humans is true?

- **A)** Oogenesis takes place within the oviduct
- **B)** Cytokinesis is unequal, producing one large ovum and polar bodies
- **C)** The oocyte released from the ovary during ovulation is fully mature
- **D)** Oogonia can reproduce mitotically within an adult female ovary

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation)**: Both meiotic divisions in oogenesis feature unequal cytokinesis, yielding one large functional cell and tiny polar bodies.

### Question 15

A peak in which hormone triggers ovulation around the 14th day of a 28-day monthly cycle?

- **A)** Estrogen
- **B)** FSH
- **C)** Progesterone
- **D)** HCG
- **E)** LH

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: The LH surge (peak in luteinizing hormone) directly triggers ovulation approximately 24-36 hours later.

### Question 16

After ovulation, the Graafian follicle regresses into the:

- **A)** Corpus atresia
- **B)** Corpus callosum
- **C)** Corpus luteum
- **D)** Corpus albicans

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Following ovulation, the remaining granulosa and theca interna cells transform into the corpus luteum under LH influence.

### Question 17

Immediately after ovulation, the mammalian egg is covered by a membrane known as:

- **A)** Chorion
- **B)** Zona pellucida
- **C)** Corona radiata
- **D)** Vitelline membrane

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The ovulated secondary oocyte is directly surrounded by the thick glycoprotein coat called the zona pellucida (with corona radiata cells on the outside).

A human female reaches menopause around the age of:

- **A)** 50 years
- **B)** 15 years

### Question 19

The vas deferens receives the duct from the seminal vesicle and opens into the prostatic urethra as the:

- **A)** Epididymis
- **B)** Ejaculatory duct
- **C)** Efferent ductule
- **D)** Ureter

### Question 20

A mature Graafian follicle is generally present in the ovary of a healthy human female around:

- **A)** 5-8 day of menstrual cycle
- **B)** 11-17 day of menstrual cycle
- **C)** 18-23 day of menstrual cycle
- **D)** 24-28 day of menstrual cycle

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Follicular maturation progresses through the follicular phase, with the mature pre-ovulatory Graafian follicle present around days 11-14 (11-17).

### Question 21

Which one of the following is NOT a male accessory genital gland?

- **A)** Seminal vesicle
- **B)** Ampulla of ductus deferens
- **C)** Prostate gland
- **D)** Bulbourethral gland

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The accessory sex glands of the male reproductive system are the seminal vesicles, prostate gland, and bulbourethral (Cowper's) glands. The ampulla is an expansion of the ductus deferens.

### Question 22

Which among the following has 23 chromosomes?

- **A)** Spermatogonia
- **B)** Zygote
- **C)** Secondary oocyte
- **D)** Oogonia

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Secondary oocyte has completed meiosis I and is haploid with 23 double-chromatid chromosomes.

### Question 23

The oocyte finishes its second meiotic division:

- **A)** Immediately before ovulation
- **B)** Immediately after ovulation
- **C)** At the time of fertilization by sperm
- **D)** Prenatally
- **E)** Before puberty

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: The secondary oocyte completes meiosis II only upon fertilization by a spermatozoon, forming the definitive ovum and second polar body.

### Question 24

Concerning the luteinizing hormone (LH), choose the CORRECT statement:

- **A)** It is responsible for follicle development
- **B)** A surge in luteinizing hormone causes the ovaries to release an egg during ovulation
- **C)** If fertilization occurs, LH / hCG will stimulate the corpus luteum to produce progesterone
- **D)** It is responsible for release of FSH
- **E)** Both B & C

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: LH surge triggers ovulation, and continued luteotropic signaling maintains the corpus luteum to secrete progesterone.

### Question 25

Regarding oogenesis, choose the FALSE statement:

- **A)** It starts during fetal life
- **B)** It is completed during puberty
- **C)** It continues till menopause
- **D)** Primary oocytes are arrested in prophase I
- **E)** The second meiotic division is completed if fertilization occurs

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Oogenesis is NOT completed during puberty; only individual follicles mature cyclically after puberty, and meiosis II finishes only if fertilization occurs.
