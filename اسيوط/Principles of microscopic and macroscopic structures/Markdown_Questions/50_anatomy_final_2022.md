### Question 1

What are the 2 types of corpus luteum and mention their duration of existence?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1) Corpus luteum of menstruation: It degenerates 10-14 days after ovulation. 2) Corpus luteum of pregnancy: persists for 4-5 months.

### Question 2

Name the results of fertilization and where does it take place?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Results of fertilization: 1. Restoration of diploid number of chromosomes. 2. Determination of sex of the embryo. 3. Initiation of cleavage divisions. Site: Lateral third (ampulla) of the fallopian tube.

---

### Question 3

What is the duration of secretory phase of the uterine cycle? Name the hormone that is responsible for this phase secreted from the ovary. Mention two characteristics occurring in the endometrium.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Duration: 14 days. Hormone: Progesterone. Characteristics: 1. Endometrial glands become elongated, wide, tortuous, and distended with glycogen- and lipid-rich secretion. 2. Spiral arteries become much elongated and coiled reaching surface mucosa. 3. Endometrium becomes thickened (>5 mm), edematous, and congested.

### Question 4

Define gastrulation. What is the evidence of occurrence of gastrulation?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Definition: The process that establishes all three germ layers (ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm) in the embryo. Evidence: The appearance of the primitive streak on the dorsal surface of the epiblast.

### Question 6

Define neurulation and enumerate two derivatives of the ectodermal germ layer.

### Question 7

What is meant by exocytosis?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Bulk movement of macromolecules out of the cell by fusion of secretory vesicles with the plasma membrane.

---

### Question 8

Define cell inclusions.

**Explanation**: Cytoplasmic deposits filled with stored macromolecules and accumulated metabolites with little or no metabolic activity themselves (e.g., glycogen, lipid droplets, pigments).
