### Question 66

4. Lipofuscin pigment: Endogenous wear-and-tear pigment
5. Lysosomes: Digestion of intracellular and foreign materials
6. Apoptosis: Normal programmed cell death
7. Golgi apparatus: Packaging, modification, and sorting of proteins
8. Microvilli: Increase surface area for absorption
9. Rough endoplasmic reticulum: Synthesis of proteins for export
10. Cilia: Motility and transportation of fluids/particles
