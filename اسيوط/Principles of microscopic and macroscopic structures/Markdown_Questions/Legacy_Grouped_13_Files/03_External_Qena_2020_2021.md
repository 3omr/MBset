## Part 1: Qena Final Exam 2020 - 2021 (Written Questions)

### Question 1 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the main functions of integral membrane proteins.

**Model Answer**:
- Transport of molecules across membrane (channels, carriers, pumps)
- Receptors for chemical signals
- Cell adhesion molecules (CAMs)
- Enzymes catalyzing membrane-associated reactions
- Structural anchors linking cytoskeleton to extracellular matrix

---

### Question 2 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the structural components of cytoskeleton.

**Model Answer**:
- Microfilaments (actin filaments)
- Intermediate filaments
- Microtubules

---

### Question 3 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the types of cartilages and mention one site for each.

**Model Answer**:
- Hyaline cartilage: Articular surfaces of long bones, costal cartilages, respiratory tract
- Elastic cartilage: Ear pinna, external auditory meatus, epiglottis
- Fibrocartilage: Intervertebral discs, pubic symphysis, menisci of knee

---

### Question 4 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the types of simple epithelium.

**Model Answer**:
- Simple squamous epithelium
- Simple cuboidal epithelium
- Simple columnar epithelium
- Pseudostratified columnar epithelium

---

### Question 5 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the microtubule-containing organelles / structures.

**Model Answer**:
- Centrioles / Centrosome
- Cilia
- Flagella
- Mitotic spindle

---

### Question 6 (Written)

**Question**: Enumerate the types of chromosomes according to the position of centromere.

**Model Answer**:
- Metacentric
- Submetacentric
- Acrocentric
- Telocentric
