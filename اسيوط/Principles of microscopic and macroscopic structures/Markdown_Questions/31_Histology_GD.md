### Question 1

Which type of cartilage forms the articular cartilage of the knee joint?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Hyaline cartilage (articular cartilage). Note: the menisci are made of fibrocartilage.

### Question 2

The mature resting cell of cartilage embedded within a lacuna is called a:

**Correct Answer**: -

### Question 3

Most hyaline and elastic cartilages are covered by a vascular connective tissue capsule called the:

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Perichondrium (absent on articular surfaces and fibrocartilage).

### Question 4

A 42-year-old woman presents with a spontaneous fracture. Light microscopy of the bone reveals bony trabeculae covered by a thicker than normal layer of uncalcified osteoid. Which best describes the underlying cause of osteomalacia?

- **A)** Decreased new bone formation by osteoblasts
- **B)** Enhanced bone resorption by activated osteoclasts
- **C)** Impaired mineralization of newly formed bone matrix
- **D)** Impaired osteocyte activity
- **E)** Increased calcification of bone matrix

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Osteomalacia (adult rickets) is characterized by defective mineralization of osteoid due to vitamin D or calcium deficiency, resulting in accumulated unmineralized bone matrix.
