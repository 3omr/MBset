### Question 1

Glycogen stored within cells is classified as a:

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Cell inclusion (non-living carbohydrate storage product).

---

### Question 2

Which histochemical stain is specifically used to demonstrate glycogen in histological sections?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Periodic acid-Schiff (PAS) reaction or Best's carmine stain.

---

### Question 3

Name two major organs that store large amounts of glycogen:

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Liver (hepatocytes) and skeletal muscle.

---

### Question 4

Define cell inclusions:

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Non-living, non-metabolizing accumulations of metabolites, stored nutrients (glycogen, lipids), or pigments within the cytoplasm.

---

### Question 5

In a cell, the synthesis of histone proteins was artificially blocked. Which cell structure will be damaged?

- **A)** Nucleolus
- **B)** Nuclear chromatin
- **C)** Golgi apparatus
- **D)** Cell membrane
- **E)** Nuclear envelope

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Histones are essential basic proteins that package DNA into nucleosomes, forming chromatin.

---

### Question 6

While examining amniotic fluid collected by amniocentesis, cells containing sex chromatin (Barr bodies) were detected. What does this indicate?

- **A)** Development of a female fetus
- **B)** Development of a male fetus
- **C)** Genetic disorder in embryonic development

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: A Barr body represents the facultative heterochromatin of an inactivated X chromosome, normally present only in somatic cells of genetic females (46,XX).

---

### Question 7

In a histological specimen, a human somatic cell is observed in the metaphase of mitotic cell division. How many chromosomes form the metaphase plate, taking into account that each chromosome contains two sister chromatids?

- **A)** 48
- **B)** 92
- **C)** 23
- **D)** 46
- **E)** 24

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Human somatic cells have 46 chromosomes (each composed of two sister chromatids joined at the centromere during metaphase).

---

### Question 8

A specimen of human skin includes a cell in which fully condensed chromosomes are aligned in the equatorial plane. Which phase of the mitotic cycle is the cell in?

- **A)** Interphase
- **B)** Late telophase
- **C)** Prophase
- **D)** Early telophase
- **E)** Metaphase

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Chromosomes line up along the equatorial (metaphase) plate during metaphase of mitosis.
