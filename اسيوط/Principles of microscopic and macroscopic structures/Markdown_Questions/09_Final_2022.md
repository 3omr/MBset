### Question 1

Define the basement membrane.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Is a thin extracellular sheet of macromolecules between the basal surface of epithelia and the underlying connective tissue.

---

### Question 2

Define myoepithelium.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Is a special type of epithelial cells that are modified to contract.

---

### Question 3

What is the function of gap junctions in cardiac muscle cells?

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: In cardiac muscle cells small ions move rapidly through gap junctions, providing electrical coupling of cardiac muscle cells, permitting synchronous contraction.

---

### Question 4

Compare between exocytosis and endocytosis.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Exocytosis: Means bulk movement of macromolecules out of the cell by fusion of secretory vesicles with the plasma membrane. Endocytosis: Means bulk movement of macromolecules into the cell by formation of vesicles at the plasma membrane.

---

### Question 5

Enumerate functions of smooth endoplasmic reticulum.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Lipid synthesis (especially steroids). 2. Detoxification of drugs (also helps muscular contraction, intracellular transport, glycogenolysis, mineral metabolism).

---

### Question 6

Enumerate stains used for staining of mitochondria.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Special stain: iron haematoxylin. Vital stain: Janus green (or histochemical detection of cytochrome oxidase enzyme).

---

### Question 7

Enumerate sites of heterochromatin in the nucleus.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: 1. Chromatin associated with nuclear envelope (peripheral chromatin). 2. Chromatin associated with nucleolus (nucleolus-associated chromatin), sex chromatin (Barr body), or clumps of heterochromatin scattered in nucleoplasm.

---

### Question 8

Enumerate types of chromosomes according to the position of the centromere.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Metacentric, Submetacentric, Acrocentric, Telocentric.

---

### Question 9

Enumerate changes occurring in cells during apoptosis.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Condensation of chromatin / destruction of DNA, cell volume decreases / blebbing of plasma membrane, and fragmentation into apoptotic bodies.

---

### Question 10

Enumerate characters of stem cells.

**Correct Answer**: -
**Explanation**: Self-renewal, small population, divide infrequently, asymmetric division, potency, plasticity.

---

### Question 11
