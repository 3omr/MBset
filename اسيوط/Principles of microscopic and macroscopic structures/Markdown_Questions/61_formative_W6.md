### Question 1

Amniotic fluid:

- **A)** Consists of 80% water and 20% solids
- **B)** About 2 liters
- **C)** Has a static nature
- **D)** Has a high protein content
- **E)** The fetal kidney is a source for its formation

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Amniotic fluid consists of 99% water and from the 5th month onward, fetal urine contributes significantly to its volume.

### Question 2

Appearance of blood vessels within the villous core is a characteristic feature of:

- **A)** Intraembryonic mesoderm
- **B)** Primary villi
- **C)** Secondary villi
- **D)** Tertiary villi
- **E)** Extraembryonic mesoderm

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Tertiary villi (definitive placental villi) are characterized by the appearance of capillaries and blood vessels within their mesenchymal core.

### Question 3

At the beginning of the third month, the head constitutes approximately:

- **A)** One fourth of the CHL
- **B)** One sixth of the CHL
- **C)** One third of the CHL
- **D)** Has no relation to the CHL
- **E)** Half of the CRL

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: At the beginning of the third month (9 weeks), the head constitutes approximately half of the crown-rump length (CRL).

### Question 4

Fraternal twins are:

- **A)** Vanishing
- **B)** Twin-to-twin transfusion
- **C)** Dizygotic
- **D)** Monozygotic
- **E)** Conjoined

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Fraternal (non-identical) twins are dizygotic, resulting from the fertilization of two separate oocytes by two separate spermatozoa.

### Question 5

Lacunae in between successive bone lamellae contain which one of the followings?

- **A)** Osteogenic cells
- **B)** Osteocytes
- **C)** Osteoblasts
- **D)** Osteoclasts
- **E)** Chondroblasts

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Osteocytes occupy the lacunae situated between adjacent bone lamellae in both compact and spongy bone.

### Question 6

The fetus has a good chance of surviving if he is born:

- **A)** Only after completion of 9th month
- **B)** During the 6th month
- **C)** Only during the 8th month
- **D)** During or after the 7th month
- **E)** Before the 6th month

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: At 28 weeks (end of 7th month), the lungs and central nervous system have developed sufficiently to give a viable fetus a high probability of survival.

### Question 7

The first attachment of the connecting stalk is to which part of the developing embryo?

- **A)** Cranial end
- **B)** Ventral surface
- **C)** Dorsal surface
- **D)** No constant attachment
- **E)** Caudal end

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: The connecting stalk initially attaches to the caudal end of the embryonic shield; later, following craniocaudal folding, its attachment shifts to the ventral aspect (umbilical region).

### Question 8

The matrix of hyaline cartilage contains:

- **A)** Aggrecan, hyaluronic acid, and blood vessels
- **B)** Aggrecan, hyaluronic acid, water and type I collagen
- **C)** Aggrecan, and water molecules
- **D)** Aggrecan, and hyaluronic acid molecules
- **E)** Aggrecan, chondronectin, water and type II collagen

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Hyaline cartilage matrix is predominantly composed of type II collagen fibrils, aggrecan proteoglycan aggregates, chondronectin, and up to 70-80% water (avascular).

### Question 9

The placenta is developed from:

- **A)** Chorion laeve and decidua basalis
- **B)** Decidua parietalis and chorion laeve
- **C)** Only from the decidua basalis
- **D)** Decidua capsularis and chorion frondosum
- **E)** Chorion frondosum and decidua basalis

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: The placenta has two components: a fetal portion derived from the chorion frondosum, and a maternal portion derived from the decidua basalis.

### Question 10

Which type of bone formation is formed from a cartilage template?

- **A)** Intraosseous
- **B)** Intramembranous
- **C)** Appositional growth
- **D)** Mesenchymal
- **E)** Intracartilaginous (endochondral)

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Intracartilaginous (endochondral) ossification involves the replacement of a pre-existing hyaline cartilage model by bone tissue.
