### Question 1

An extracellular structure lies at the interface of epithelial cells and underlying connective tissue. Nutrients for avascular epithelial cells must diffuse across this structure. What is the name of this structure?

- **A)** Microvilli
- **B)** Gap junctions
- **C)** Cilia
- **D)** Cell layers
- **E)** Basement membrane

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: The basement membrane separates epithelium from connective tissue and serves as a selective filtration barrier and structural support.

### Question 2

An 11-month-old girl is referred to a pediatric gastroenterology clinic due to a history of generalized weakness, slow growth, and refractory diarrhea. Examination of the small intestinal epithelium confirms failure to absorb nutrients due to a significant decrease in which of the following?

- **A)** Microvilli
- **B)** Cilia
- **C)** Gap junctions
- **D)** Basement membrane thickness
- **E)** Cell layers

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Microvilli form the brush border of intestinal enterocytes; their loss or blunting causes severe nutrient malabsorption.

### Question 3

A 23-year-old non-smoking man presents with a 5-year history of chronic respiratory infections (bronchiectasis). A bronchial biopsy indicates altered ciliary structures in epithelial cells. Which structure is involved?

- **A)** Hemidesmosomes
- **B)** Cilia
- **C)** Basolateral membrane folds
- **D)** Microvilli
- **E)** Tight junctions

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Primary ciliary dyskinesia (Kartagener / immotile cilia syndrome) results from defects in ciliary dynein arms, leading to impaired mucociliary clearance and recurrent respiratory infections.

### Question 4

Using immunohistochemistry, a population of cells is shown to be positive for the protein connexin. From this we can infer that the cells are connected by which type of junction?

- **A)** Tight (occluding) junctions
- **B)** Zonula adherens
- **C)** Gap junctions
- **D)** Hemidesmosomes
- **E)** Desmosomes (macula adherens)

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Gap junctions (communicating junctions) are composed of hexameric complexes of transmembrane proteins called connexins.

### Question 5

Exocrine glands in which the secretory acini all produce a secretion of heavily glycosylated, hydrophilic proteins are classified as:

- **A)** Serous gland
- **B)** Mixed gland
- **C)** Mucous gland
- **D)** Tubuloacinar gland
- **E)** Simple gland

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Mucous glands secrete mucins (heavily glycosylated proteins that hydrate to form lubricating mucus).
