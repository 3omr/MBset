### Question 1

A 26-year-old woman experiences episodes of bright red vaginal bleeding at week 28, week 32, and week 34 of pregnancy. The bleeding spontaneously subsided each time. Using ultrasound, the placenta is located in the lower right portion of the uterus over the internal os. What is the diagnosis?

- **A)** Placenta previa
- **B)** Hydatidiform mole
- **C)** Vasa previa
- **D)** Premature rupture of the amniotic membrane
- **E)** Toxemia of pregnancy

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Placenta previa is the implantation of the placenta in the lower uterine segment over or adjacent to the internal cervical os, presenting with painless, bright red vaginal bleeding in the third trimester.

### Question 2

After ovulation, the ovum lives for how long, after which it degenerates?

- **A)** 1-2 days
- **B)** 6 hours
- **C)** 5-6 days
- **D)** 1-2 weeks
- **E)** 1 month

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: After ovulation, the secondary oocyte remains viable and capable of fertilization for approximately 24 hours (1-2 days).

### Question 3

An 11-month-old girl is referred to a pediatric clinic due to a history of generalized weakness, slow growth, and repeated diarrhea. Microscopic examination of the epithelium lining her small intestine confirms that the failure to absorb nutrients is most likely due to a significant decrease in which of the following?

- **A)** Basement membrane thickness
- **B)** Cell layers
- **C)** Microvilli
- **D)** Gap junctions
- **E)** Cilia

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Celiac disease and malabsorption enteropathies lead to blunting/loss of intestinal villi and microvilli (brush border), severely reducing the absorptive surface area.

### Question 4

Capacitation of the sperm:

- **A)** Removes the head of the sperm
- **B)** Is caused by the zona pellucida
- **C)** Prevents polyspermy
- **D)** Occurs in the male genital tract
- **E)** Occurs in the female genital tract

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Capacitation is the functional maturation of spermatozoa that occurs within the female reproductive tract (uterus and fallopian tubes), requiring about 7 hours.

### Question 5

Polarity is a character which appears in:

- **A)** Stratified columnar epithelium
- **B)** Simple epithelium
- **C)** Keratinized epithelium
- **D)** Transitional epithelium
- **E)** Stratified squamous epithelium

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Epithelial polarity (apical, lateral, and basal domains) is typically prominent in simple epithelia where single cell layers interface directly with lumen and basement membrane.

### Question 6

Primordial ovarian follicle is:

- **A)** Primary oocyte is surrounded by theca cells
- **B)** Mature ovum surrounded by follicular cells
- **C)** Secondary oocyte surrounded by follicular cells
- **D)** Primary oocyte surrounded by granulosa cells
- **E)** Primary oocyte surrounded by flat follicular cells

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: A primordial follicle consists of a primary oocyte arrested in prophase I, surrounded by a single layer of flattened follicular (stromal) cells.

### Question 7

Which of the following is NOT an anchoring junction?

- **A)** Zonula adherens
- **B)** Desmosomes
- **C)** Macula adherens
- **D)** Hemidesmosomes
- **E)** Zonula occludens

**Correct Answer**: E
**Explanation**: Zonula occludens (tight junction) is an occluding/barrier junction, whereas zonula adherens, macula adherens (desmosomes), and hemidesmosomes are anchoring junctions.

### Question 8

Which of the following statements are TRUE about menstruation?

- **A)** During menstruation only the functional layer of the endometrium is shed, with the basal layer remaining intact
- **B)** During menstruation the basal layer of the endometrium is shed, with the functional layer remaining intact
- **C)** During menstruation the entire endometrium is shed
- **D)** The secretory phase ends by the time of ovulation
- **E)** Absence of menstruation always indicates an active pregnancy

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: During menstruation, the stratum functionale (compact and spongy layers) of the endometrium is shed, while the stratum basale is preserved to regenerate the lining.

### Question 9

Which of the following structures is not necessary for blastocyst implantation?

- **A)** Functional layer of endometrium
- **B)** Zona pellucida
- **C)** Syncytiotrophoblast
- **D)** Endometrium in progestational phase
- **E)** Cytotrophoblast

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: The blastocyst must hatch from the zona pellucida (zona hatching) before implantation can occur; therefore the zona pellucida is NOT present during implantation.

### Question 10

Which type of epithelium is specialized in moving particles across its surface and is found in airways?

- **A)** Stratified columnar epithelium
- **B)** Pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium
- **C)** Simple squamous epithelium
- **D)** Stratified squamous epithelium
- **E)** Transitional epithelium

**Correct Answer**: B
**Explanation**: Pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium (respiratory epithelium) lines most of the conducting respiratory tract and moves mucus and trapped particles toward the pharynx.
