### Question 1

Primary spermatocytes arise as a result of:

- **A)** First meiosis of spermatogonia
- **B)** Second meiosis of spermatogonia
- **C)** None of the above
- **D)** Simple mitosis of spermatogonia

**Correct Answer**: D
**Explanation**: Type B spermatogonia undergo mitosis to produce diploid primary spermatocytes.

### Question 2

A spermatid contains:

- **A)** 22 + X or 22 + Y
- **B)** 44 + XY
- **C)** 22 + XY
- **D)** 44 + XX

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Spermatids are haploid cells resulting from meiosis II and contain 23 chromosomes (22 autosomes + X or Y).

### Question 3

In spermiogenesis, Golgi bodies give rise to:

- **A)** Acrosomal cap
- **B)** Mitochondrial sheath
- **C)** Tail of the sperm
- **D)** Axial filament

**Explanation**: The acrosome of the spermatozoon is derived from the Golgi apparatus during spermiogenesis.

- **A)** 3-4 days
- **B)** 120 days
- **C)** 64 days

### Question 5

Spermatogenesis is regulated by:

- **A)** Testosterone
- **B)** Growth hormone
- **C)** FSH and LH
- **D)** None of the above

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Spermatogenesis is regulated by the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, specifically FSH (acts on Sertoli cells) and LH (stimulates Leydig cells to produce testosterone).

### Question 6

Aspermia means:

- **A)** No motile sperms
- **B)** No sperms in semen
- **C)** Complete absence of semen
- **D)** Low sperm count

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**: Aspermia is the complete lack of semen with ejaculation, whereas azoospermia is the absence of spermatozoa in the semen.

### Question 8

The process of spermatogenesis includes:

- **A)** All of the above
- **B)** Spermatocytogenesis
- **C)** Meiosis
- **D)** Spermiogenesis

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**: Spermatogenesis encompasses spermatocytogenesis (mitosis of spermatogonia), meiosis (production of haploid spermatids), and spermiogenesis (transformation into mature spermatozoa).

### Question 46

- **B)** Increase in the size of the cells
