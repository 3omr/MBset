## Quiz weeks 4–5

### Q1: Which is a function of the endoplasmic reticulum?

- **A)** Destroy old cell parts
- **B)** Synthesize proteins
- **C)** Generate cellular power
- **D)** Contain oxidative enzymes

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** The source marks protein synthesis. Rough endoplasmic reticulum synthesizes proteins destined for secretion, membranes, or specific organelles.

---

### Q2: Which organelle packages proteins and sends them out of the cell to other cells?

- **A)** Endoplasmic reticulum
- **B)** Ribosome
- **C)** Golgi body
- **D)** Lysosome

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts, and packages proteins for delivery to cellular destinations or secretion.

---

### Q3: Phospholipids are the only type of lipid in the plasma membrane.

- **A)** True
- **B)** False

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** The plasma membrane also contains cholesterol and glycolipids, in addition to phospholipids.

### Q4: Gated channels can be opened or closed by which three types of stimulus?

**Correct Answer:** -
