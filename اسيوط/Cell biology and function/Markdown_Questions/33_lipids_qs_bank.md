## Define

### Q1: Define ketolysis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Ketolysis is the oxidation of ketone bodies to acetyl-CoA for energy production; acetyl-CoA is then oxidized to carbon dioxide and water in the citric acid cycle.

---

### Q2: Define ketosis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Ketosis is an increased concentration of ketone bodies in the blood (ketonemia) and their presence in the urine (ketonuria).

---

## Mention

### Q3: Describe how glycerol 3-phosphate is synthesized in hepatic and extrahepatic tissues.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** In adipose tissue and liver, dihydroxyacetone phosphate from glycolysis is reduced to glycerol 3-phosphate by glycerol 3-phosphate dehydrogenase. The liver can also phosphorylate free glycerol using glycerol kinase.

---

### Q4: What is the biochemical role of thiophorase?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Thiophorase transfers CoA from succinyl-CoA to acetoacetate, forming acetoacetyl-CoA during ketolysis.

---

### Q5: What is the source of CoA when acetoacetate is activated during ketolysis?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Succinyl-CoA donates CoA to acetoacetate in the thiophorase reaction.

---

## Enumerate

### Q6: List the two fatty-acid-synthesis reactions that use NADPH.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Reduction of the β-ketoacyl intermediate by β-ketoacyl-ACP reductase, and reduction of the enoyl intermediate by enoyl-ACP reductase.

---

### Q7: List the ketone bodies.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate, and acetone.

---

### Q8: List the requirements for fatty-acid synthesis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source lists acetyl-CoA, NADPH + H⁺, and the fatty-acid-synthase complex.

---

### Q9: List the sources of NADPH + H⁺.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The pentose-phosphate pathway, cytosolic isocitrate dehydrogenase, and malic enzyme.

---

### Q10: List conditions that increase ketogenesis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Fasting or starvation, a carbohydrate-poor diet, a high-fat diet, diabetes mellitus, strenuous muscular exercise, and pregnancy.

---

### Q11: List the components or activities of the fatty-acid-synthase complex.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Ketoacyl synthase, malonyl/acetyl transacylase, β-ketoacyl reductase, hydratase, enoyl reductase, thioesterase, and the acyl carrier protein (ACP).

**EXP:** The source lists glucose metabolism, fatty-acid oxidation, ketone-body oxidation, and amino-acid catabolism.

## MCQs

### Q13: Acetyl-CoA required for de novo fatty-acid synthesis is obtained from which source?

- **A)** Fatty-acid oxidation
- **B)** Acetate
- **C)** Pyruvate
- **D)** Ketone bodies

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Pyruvate is converted to acetyl-CoA by the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex. Mitochondrial acetyl units are transferred to the cytosol mainly as citrate for fatty-acid synthesis.
