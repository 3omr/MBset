### Q1: A 69-year-old alcoholic man who lives alone is admitted for evaluation of a shoulder wound that is not healing well. Numerous ecchymoses are noted on the posterior legs and thighs. Examination reveals minute hemorrhages around hair follicles and splinter hemorrhages in the nail beds. Hemoglobin is 10 g/dL, with no other hematologic abnormalities. What is the most appropriate therapy?

- **A)** Factor VIII
- **B)** Iron
- **C)** Vitamin B12
- **D)** Vitamin C
- **E)** Vitamin K
