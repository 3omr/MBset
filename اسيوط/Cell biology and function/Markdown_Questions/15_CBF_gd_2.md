**Answer Source:** derived

### Q3: Clinical case: A 50-year-old female presents to your clinic with excessive thirst, fluid intake, and urination. She denies urinary tract infection symptoms and reports no known medical problems, but has not seen a doctor in many years. She is obese and in no acute distress; the rest of her physical examination is normal. Urinalysis shows large amounts of glucose, and her random serum glucose is 320 mg/dL. What is the biochemical basis of this disease?

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**Source Question:** Case prompt 3
**EXP:** Type 2 diabetes develops from insulin resistance in skeletal muscle, adipose tissue, and liver, together with progressive beta-cell dysfunction and inadequate insulin secretion. Reduced peripheral glucose uptake and failure to suppress hepatic glucose production cause hyperglycemia. Glycosuria produces osmotic diuresis, while increased free-fatty-acid flux contributes to dyslipidemia.

### Q4: Clinical case: A 50-year-old female presents to your clinic with excessive thirst, fluid intake, and urination. She denies urinary tract infection symptoms and reports no known medical problems, but has not seen a doctor in many years. She is obese and in no acute distress; the rest of her physical examination is normal. Urinalysis shows large amounts of glucose, and her random serum glucose is 320 mg/dL. What investigations can help diagnose and monitor this disease?

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**Source Question:** Case prompt 4
**EXP:** Diagnosis can use fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c, a 2-hour 75-g oral glucose tolerance test, or random plasma glucose with classic symptoms. A random value of at least 200 mg/dL with symptoms is diagnostic; this patient’s value is 320 mg/dL. Monitoring includes HbA1c and home blood-glucose monitoring or continuous glucose monitoring as appropriate, plus screening for complications with urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio and eGFR, retinal examination, foot and neuropathy assessment, and lipid profile.
