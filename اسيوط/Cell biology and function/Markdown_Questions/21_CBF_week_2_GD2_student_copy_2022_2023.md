### Q3: Serum alkaline phosphatase level increases in which condition?

- **A)** Carcinoma of the prostate
- **B)** Hypothyroidism
- **C)** Myocardial ischemia
- **D)** Osteosarcoma

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Osteosarcoma increases osteoblastic activity and can markedly elevate alkaline phosphatase. Prostate carcinoma can also elevate it when there are osteoblastic bone metastases.
**Source Question:** 3
**Source Pages:** 1
**Note:** Both A (with osteoblastic metastases) and D can raise alkaline phosphatase; D is the most direct answer.
