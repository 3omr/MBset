### Q1: An 82-year-old man has progressive memory impairment, cognitive decline, and loss of independent function. Brain autopsy shows extracellular neuritic plaques and inter-neuronal tangles. Discuss the biochemical basis of the disease.

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**Source Question:** Alzheimer case, Q1
**EXP:** Alzheimer disease involves abnormal protein processing and aggregation. Amyloid-beta peptides aggregate extracellularly into neuritic plaques, while hyperphosphorylated tau aggregates intracellularly into neurofibrillary tangles. These aggregates disrupt synaptic function and neuronal transport, contribute to neuronal injury and loss, and underlie progressive cognitive decline.

### Q8: Negative nitrogen balance is seen in all of the following conditions except:

- **A)** Pregnancy
- **B)** Growth
- **C)** Fever
- **D)** Convalescence

**Correct Answer:** A
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Pregnancy is associated with positive nitrogen balance. Growth and convalescence are also positive-balance states, so the source item has multiple valid exceptions.
**Source Question:** Bio-case 1.1, Q7
**Source Pages:** 2
**Note:** Pregnancy is selected as the single platform answer; Growth and Convalescence also fit “except.”
