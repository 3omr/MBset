## Quiz weeks 7–8

### Q1: Vitamins are what?

- **A)** Generally synthesized in the body
- **B)** Proteins in nature
- **C)** Produced in endocrine glands
- **D)** Accessory food factors

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** The source marks "accessory food factors." Vitamins are organic micronutrients required in small amounts for normal metabolism.

### Q2: Which vitamin is required for the formation of hydroxyproline in collagen?

### Q6: Deficiency of which vitamin increases blood concentrations of pyruvic and lactic acids?

**Answer Source:** marked
