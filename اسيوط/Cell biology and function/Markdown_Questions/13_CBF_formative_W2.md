### Q1: A chronic alcoholic, while out on a binge, became very confused and forgetful. The police brought him to the emergency department. On examination, he displayed nystagmus and ataxia. Which enzyme is displaying reduced activity in his brain under these conditions?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** α-Ketoglutarate dehydrogenase is thiamine pyrophosphate-dependent and can have reduced activity in thiamine deficiency associated with chronic alcohol misuse. Other thiamine-dependent enzymes may also be affected.

### Q2: A biochemistry graduate student isolates all the enzymes of the TCA cycle and adds oxaloacetate, acetyl-CoA, appropriate energy precursors, cofactors, and water. Which of the following will not be a direct product of this experiment?

- **A)** FADH₂
- **B)** ATP
- **C)** NADH
- **D)** CO₂
- **E)** GTP

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** In the usual teaching convention, the TCA cycle directly generates NADH, FADH₂, CO₂, and GTP at the succinyl-CoA synthetase step, but not ATP. Some succinyl-CoA synthetase isoforms can form ATP, so the presence of both ATP and GTP makes the item dependent on the assumed isoform; B is the conventional single-best answer.
**Source Pages:** 2
**Tag:** Department, Formative, Week 2

### Q3: A young infant nourished with a synthetic formula has a serum and urine sugar that gives a positive reducing-sugar test but a negative glucose-oxidase test. Acid hydrolysis does not increase the amount of reducing sugar. Which compound is most likely present in the infant's urine and serum?

- **A)** Maltose
- **B)** Lactose
- **C)** Glucose
- **D)** Sorbitol
- **E)** Fructose

**Correct Answer:** E
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Fructose is a reducing monosaccharide but is not detected by a glucose-specific oxidase assay. Because acid hydrolysis does not increase the reducing-sugar measurement, the sugar is not a disaccharide that releases additional reducing monosaccharides.
**Source Pages:** 3–4

### Q4: A 47-year-old obese man is diagnosed with insulin-resistant diabetes mellitus (type 2). If his symptoms are due to a problem at the level of the glucose transporter, which tissue listed below will be most affected?

- **A)** Muscle
- **B)** Liver
- **C)** Brain
- **D)** Small intestine

**Correct Answer:** A
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Skeletal muscle glucose uptake is strongly insulin-dependent through GLUT4 translocation. A defect in this pathway therefore impairs muscle glucose uptake; hepatic GLUT2-mediated transport is not insulin-dependent in the same way.
**Source Pages:** 5–6
**Tag:** Department, Formative, Week 2

### Q7: Compared with the resting state, vigorously contracting skeletal muscle shows which change?

- **A)** Increased oxygen availability
- **B)** Decreased levels of fructose 2,6-bisphosphate
- **C)** Increased reduction of pyruvate to lactate
- **D)** Decreased AMP/ATP ratio

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** During vigorous contraction, glycolytic flux rises and lactate dehydrogenase reduces more pyruvate to lactate, regenerating NAD⁺ so glycolysis can continue when oxygen delivery is insufficient for the workload.
**Source Pages:** 9
**Tag:** Department, Formative, Week 2

### Q8: The refilling of TCA-cycle intermediates is frequently dependent upon which cofactor?

- **A)** Niacin
- **B)** Carnitine
- **C)** Lipoate
- **D)** Riboflavin
- **E)** Pyridoxal phosphate

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Anaplerotic carboxylation reactions that replenish TCA-cycle intermediates commonly require biotin, which is absent. Lipoate is a cofactor for oxidative decarboxylation, not anaplerotic carboxylation.
**Source Question:** 8
**Source Pages:** 10

### Q9: What is leptin resistance?

- **A)** Leptin deficiency caused by a mutation in the leptin gene
- **B)** Low leptin concentration with increased energy intake
- **C)** High leptin concentration without a reduction in energy intake
- **D)** Lack of leptin release from adipose tissue

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Leptin resistance describes a reduced physiological response to leptin despite circulating leptin, often at high concentrations. Appetite and energy intake therefore fail to decrease as expected.

### Q10: When neuropeptide Y is injected into the hypothalamus or brain ventricles, it produces which response?

- **C)** Cessation of eating behavior

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** derived

### Q11: Which of the following accounts for the largest component of daily energy expenditure in a sedentary individual?

### Q12: Which of the following statements is true regarding basal metabolic rate?

- **A)** It is not affected by energy expenditure.
- **B)** It is not influenced by hormonal changes.
- **C)** It increases in starvation.
- **D)** It is not affected by dietary changes.
- **E)** It may decrease by 40% in starvation.

**Correct Answer:** E
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** The source marks a decrease of up to 40% during starvation. Prolonged energy deprivation lowers basal metabolic rate through adaptive reductions in energy expenditure; the exact decrease varies with duration and severity.
