### Q1: Which is not a function of the plasma membrane?

- **A)** To facilitate cell-to-cell communication
- **B)** To generate the energy to power cell activities
- **C)** To protect the cell from the surrounding environment
- **D)** To control the rate of certain molecules entering and leaving the cell

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** The plasma membrane separates the cell from its surroundings, regulates molecular transport, and supports cell signaling. In human cells, ATP is generated mainly by mitochondria and glycolysis, not by the plasma membrane.

### Q3: The impermeability of a lipid-insoluble substance results from which of its properties?

- **B)** Shape

**Correct Answer:** C
**Answer Source:** derived

### Q7: Which statement characterizes alpha-helix regions of proteins?

- **A)** They all have the same primary structure.
- **B)** They are formed principally by hydrogen bonds between a carbonyl oxygen atom in one peptide bond and the amide hydrogen from a different peptide bond.
- **C)** They are formed principally by hydrogen bonds between a carbonyl atom in one peptide bond and hydrogen atoms on the side chain of another amino acid.
- **D)** They are formed by hydrogen bonding between two adjacent amino acids in the primary sequence.
- **E)** They require a high content of proline and glycine.

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** An alpha helix is stabilized by intrachain hydrogen bonds between the carbonyl oxygen of one peptide bond and the amide hydrogen of a peptide bond farther along the same polypeptide chain, typically four residues ahead.
**Source Pages:** 6–7
**Tag:** Department, Formative, Week 1

### Q13: A small molecule that decreases enzyme activity by binding at a site other than the catalytic site is termed which of the following?

### Q14: In competitive inhibition, an inhibitor:

- **A)** Binds at several different sites on an enzyme
- **B)** Binds reversibly at the active site
- **C)** Lowers the characteristic Vmax of the enzyme
- **D)** Binds covalently to the enzyme
- **E)** Binds only to the enzyme-substrate complex

**Correct Answer:** B
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** A competitive inhibitor reversibly competes with substrate for the enzyme's active site. Under the classical model, sufficiently high substrate concentration can overcome inhibition, increasing the apparent Km without changing Vmax.
**Source Pages:** 13–14
**Tag:** Department, Formative, Week 1
