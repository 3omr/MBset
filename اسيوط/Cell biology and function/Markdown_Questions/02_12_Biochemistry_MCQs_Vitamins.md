### Q20: Vitamin B₁₂:

- **A)** Is a potent antioxidant
- **B)** Deficiency causes pellagra
- **C)** Vegetable seeds are a major source
- **D)** Binds transcobalamin I in the liver
- **E)** Is absorbed in the stomach

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** online
**EXP:** Haptocorrin/transcobalamin I binds most circulating cobalamin and is associated with hepatic storage; active delivery to most tissues is mainly via transcobalamin II. The source statement is the best-supported option ([NCBI Bookshelf review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8886776/)).
**Source Question:** 20
**Source Pages:** 3
**Note:** Wording in D is broad; D is retained as the best-supported listed statement. See the source-backed vitamin B₁₂ transport description.
