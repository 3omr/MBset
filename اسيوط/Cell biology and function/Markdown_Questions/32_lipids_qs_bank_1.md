### Q2: State the roles of the dehydrogenases in β-oxidation.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Acyl-CoA dehydrogenase catalyzes the first oxidation step in each β-oxidation cycle. 3-Hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase oxidizes the 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA intermediate to 3-ketoacyl-CoA.

---

### Q3: State the importance of β-oxidation.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** β-oxidation produces energy and acetyl-CoA, which enters other metabolic pathways and can be used for ketone-body formation.

---

### Q4: List causes of excessive lipolysis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Starvation, diabetes mellitus, a low-carbohydrate diet, and severe catabolic states such as tuberculosis.

---

### Q5: List anti-lipolytic factors.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Insulin, nicotinic acid (which inhibits adenylyl cyclase), and prostaglandins.

## Mention

### Q6: How does carnitine participate in lipid transport?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The carnitine shuttle transfers long-chain fatty-acyl groups across the inner mitochondrial membrane into the matrix, where β-oxidation occurs. Carnitine carries the acyl group; the fatty acyl-CoA molecule itself does not cross the membrane intact.

---

### Q7: How does the body overcome a branch at the β-position of a fatty acid during β-oxidation?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** α-oxidation removes one carbon from a branched fatty acid such as phytanic acid. The shortened product can then undergo β-oxidation.

---

### Q8: What is the role of thiokinase in fatty-acid metabolism?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Thiokinase (acyl-CoA synthetase) activates a fatty acid to fatty acyl-CoA, using ATP and producing AMP and pyrophosphate.

---

### Q9: What metabolic defect causes Refsum disease?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Refsum disease is an inherited defect of phytanic-acid α-oxidation, which leads to phytanic-acid accumulation in tissues, including the nervous system.

---

**EXP:** Acyl-CoA dehydrogenase, enoyl-CoA hydratase, 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase, and 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase (ketothiolase).

## Define

### Q12: Define lipolysis.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Lipolysis is the hydrolysis of triacylglycerols in adipose tissue to glycerol and fatty acids.

---

### Q13: Define β-oxidation.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** β-oxidation is a mitochondrial pathway that removes two-carbon units from the carboxyl end of fatty acyl-CoA, producing acetyl-CoA, NADH, and FADH₂.

---

### Q14: Define α-oxidation.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** α-oxidation removes one carbon from the carboxyl end of a branched fatty acid such as phytanic acid, allowing subsequent β-oxidation when a methyl group blocks the β-oxidation pathway.

---

### Q15: Define ω-oxidation.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** ω-oxidation oxidizes the terminal methyl group of a fatty acid, converting it to a carboxyl group and producing a dicarboxylic acid.

---

## Complete

**EXP:** α-oxidation; phytanic acid.
