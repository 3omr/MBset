### Q1: When the arterial blood pressure increase:

- **A)** The arterial baroreceptors cause reflex increase in heart rate
- **B)** Atrial stretch receptors cause reflex vasoconstriction
- **C)** Atrial natriuretic peptide secretion is decreased
- **D)** Aldosterone secretion is decreased

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** A rise in ABP raises ANP (via atrial stretch) and reflexly inhibits sympathetic outflow, which suppresses renin-angiotensin-aldosterone activity, so aldosterone secretion falls; baroreceptors reflexly slow the heart, and atrial stretch increases (not decreases) ANP and causes vasodilatation
**Tag:** External, Sohag 2021
**Year:** 2021

### Q2: According to Poiseuille-Hogan law, the most important determinant of peripheral resistance is

- **A)** Blood viscosity
- **B)** The cross-sectional area of the blood vessel
- **C)** The length of blood vessels
- **D)** The blood vessel width

**Correct Answer:** D
**Answer Source:** marked
**EXP:** Resistance is inversely proportional to the 4th power of the radius, so small physiological changes in vessel width (radius) dominate over viscosity or length as the main determinant of resistance
**Tag:** External, Sohag 2021

### Q13: If the ejection fraction increases, there will be a decrease in
