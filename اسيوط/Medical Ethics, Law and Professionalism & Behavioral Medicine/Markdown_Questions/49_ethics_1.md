### Q3: Discuss the legal position regarding appointing a manager for a medical facility when its owner dies, and the rights of the owner's heirs.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** According to the source, the facility license may remain with the heirs for 20 years after the owner's death if they apply within six months. They must appoint a physician licensed to practice medicine as the facility manager and notify the administrative authority and the competent Medical Syndicate. If an heir graduates from medical school during this period, the license may be transferred to that heir, subject to the conditions described in the source. If no heir is a physician or medical student by the end of the period, the license must be transferred to a licensed physician or handled by the competent licensing authority.
**Source pages:** 5, 8

### Q6: In what circumstances may the license of a medical facility be revoked?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source lists these circumstances: the license holder requests cancellation; the facility stops operating for more than one year; the facility is moved or rebuilt; it is used for a purpose other than the one for which it was licensed; a final court order closes or removes it; violations recur despite penalties; or unlicensed people practice medicine or other medical professions there. The source also states that repeated professional violations may lead to a hospital being placed under direct Ministry supervision.
**Source pages:** 15, 17, 21

### Q7: A physician performs female genital mutilation on a 10-year-old girl in a private clinic without anesthetic medication. The child develops severe bleeding and neurogenic shock. What professional disciplinary process and penalties does the source describe?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source says physicians may be referred to a Medical Syndicate disciplinary board that includes state judges, prosecution members, and Syndicate members. A physician may defend himself or be represented by a colleague, except where the alleged error constitutes a crime causing death or disability. The listed penalties range from notice or warning to reprimand, a fine, temporary suspension for up to one year, or removal from the Syndicate register. The source does not specify a case-specific finding or penalty for this scenario.
**Source pages:** 18, 19

---

### Q8: Define a private hospital according to the source.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A private hospital is a facility prepared to receive and examine patients and has at least 15 beds. The source also requires at least two operating rooms, a recovery room, and an intensive care room, with equipment appropriate to the applicable regulations and the hospital's specialties.
**Source page:** 20

---

### Q9: What disciplinary system for physicians does the source describe?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A physician may be investigated before a Medical Syndicate disciplinary board composed of state judges, prosecution members, and Syndicate members. The physician may defend himself or be represented by a colleague, except when the alleged error constitutes a crime causing death or disability. The penalties listed are notice, warning, reprimand, a fine, temporary suspension for up to one year, or removal from the register. A physician whose name is removed may not resume practice until the Syndicate approves reinstatement.
**Source pages:** 18, 19, 24
