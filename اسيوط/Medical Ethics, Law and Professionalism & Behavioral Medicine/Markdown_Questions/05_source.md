# Source 05 — Vertical written review notes

Translated from the English/Arabic text layer. The source has three numbered sections (memory, developmental psychology, and personality); question numbers below run continuously. The source repeats the anterograde-amnesia item at the end of the developmental section, so that occurrence is retained and noted rather than silently removed.

### Q1: Define memory.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A set of mental processes that receives, encodes, stores, organizes, alters, and retrieves information over time.

---

### Q2: List the types of memory.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Sensory memory, short-term memory, and long-term memory.

---

### Q3: What is sensory memory?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** An initial process that receives and holds environmental information in its raw form for a brief period, from an instant to several seconds.

---

### Q4: What is short-term memory?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A process that holds a limited amount of information for a short time; it is the temporary storage of information.

---

### Q5: How long does short-term memory last according to the source?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Its duration is relatively limited.

---

### Q6: What is chunking?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Combining separate items of information into a larger unit, or chunk.

---

### Q7: What is long-term memory?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A process for storing almost unlimited amounts of information over long periods.

---

### Q8: What is encoding?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Getting information into the system by translating it into a neural code that the brain processes.

---

### Q9: What does storing involve?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Retaining information over time.

---

### Q10: What is retrieving?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Getting or recalling information that has been placed in short-term or long-term storage.

---

### Q11: Define amnesia.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Memory loss due to special conditions such as brain injury, illness, or psychological trauma.

---

### Q12: List the types of amnesia.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Retrograde amnesia and anterograde amnesia.

---

### Q13: What is retrograde amnesia?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Memory loss for events that took place before the onset of amnesia.

---

### Q14: What is anterograde amnesia?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Memory loss for events that occur after the initial onset of amnesia.

---

### Q15: What is cognitive development?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** How a person perceives, thinks about, and understands their world.

---

### Q16: List Piaget's four stages of cognitive development.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operations, and formal operations.

---

### Q17: What characterizes the sensorimotor stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Children relate sensory experiences to motor actions. The source gives the age range as birth to about 2 years.

---

### Q18: What characterizes the preoperational stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Children solve simple problems and think or talk about things that are not present. The source gives the age range as about 2–7 years.

---

### Q19: What characterizes the concrete-operations stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Children can perform a number of logical mental operations. The source gives the age range as about 7–11 years.

---

### Q20: What characterizes the formal-operations stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A person can think and solve abstract problems logically. The source gives the age range as about 12 years through adulthood.

---

### Q21: Which theory proposes that personality develops through eight major psychosocial stages?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Erikson's psychosocial-development theory.

---

### Q22: Describe Freud's psychosexual-stage theory.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** It proposes five developmental periods—oral, anal, phallic, latency, and genital—during which the individual seeks pleasure from different body areas associated with sexual feelings.

---

### Q23: What characterizes Freud's oral stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** During early infancy, the first 18 months of life, the infant's pleasure-seeking is centered on the mouth.

---

### Q24: What characterizes Freud's anal stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** From about 1.5 to 3 years, pleasure-seeking is centered on the anus and its elimination functions.

---

### Q25: What characterizes Freud's phallic stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** From about 3 to 6 years, the child's pleasure-seeking is centered on the genitals.

---

### Q26: What characterizes Freud's latency stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** From about age 6 to puberty, the child represses sexual thoughts and engages in nonsexual activities such as developing social and intellectual skills.

---

### Q27: What is the age range for Freud's genital stage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** From puberty through adulthood.

---

### Q29: Define personality.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The total quality of an individual's behavior as shown in characteristic habits, thought, expression, attitude, and interests.

---

### Q30: How is personality usually described?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** In terms of traits.

---

### Q31: What is an integrated personality?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** One in which several traits are combined in an effective, harmonious way.

---
