# Source 54 — Physician duties: case questions

Translated from the Arabic/English text layer. The PDF repeats each case prompt on the following page before its source answer; each distinct case is included once. Model answers are translated from the source.

### Q1: A physician finds an unusual rash on a child and sends a photograph to a dermatologist, who prescribes a corticosteroid ointment. Five days later, the child's condition worsens and the child is admitted to intensive care. Discuss the responsibility of the treating physician.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** When physicians consult one another about a patient's diagnosis or treatment, the physician directly responsible for examining and treating the patient retains responsibility. The treating physician must assess the patient's overall condition and any other symptoms or diseases that could affect the safety of the recommended treatment; a remote consultation does not replace that assessment.

---

### Q2: A child is diagnosed with meningitis at a rural health unit. Several days later, many children from the same area present with similar symptoms. Discuss the physician's responsibilities to the community and state when disclosure of patient information is permitted.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The physician should notify the competent health authorities when an epidemic disease is suspected so that preventive measures can protect the community. The physician should also warn the patient and companions about precautions and explain the risks of not following them. Patient confidentiality may be breached when required by a court order, when disclosure is necessary to prevent serious and certain harm to another person, or in other cases specified by law.

---

### Q3: Two nearby laboratories charge the same fees. A physician knows that one provides reliable, high-quality testing and that the other is negligent and does not meet appropriate standards. What should the physician do when advising patients?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A physician must not request or accept payment or any other benefit for prescribing particular medicines or devices, or for referring patients to a hospital, treatment center, nursing facility, pharmacy, laboratory, or medical-supply provider. The physician may explain the laboratories' advantages and disadvantages and leave the choice to the patient, without pressure or financial benefit.

---

### Q4: A physician appears on a television program, explains a treatment method that is still experimental, and gives the audience the addresses and telephone numbers of the physician's clinic and hospital. Evaluate this conduct and state the rules for physicians discussing medical topics in the media.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** When addressing the public about medical topics, a physician should not promote a workplace, contact details, experience, or achievements, and should state only the professional role and specialty. The explanation should use language suitable for a non-specialist audience. The physician should avoid presenting uncertain scientific opinions as established facts and should avoid discussing disputed topics that belong in specialist scientific forums.

---

### Q5: A junior anesthesiologist believes that a senior surgeon uses outdated techniques that prolong operations, cause postoperative pain, and extend treatment. The surgeon also makes comments that may embarrass nurses. Should the anesthesiologist confront the surgeon directly or report the conduct?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A physician must not disparage a colleague's abilities. Professional criticism should be presented to an impartial scientific committee. A work-related dispute should first be addressed amicably; if unresolved, it should be referred to the competent local Medical Syndicate branch, with an appeal to the General Syndicate available to either party.
