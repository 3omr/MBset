# Source 44 — Written questions on Medical Ethics

Translated from the Arabic source. Extracted 35 written questions with the model answers printed in the source. Numbered lists in model answers are preserved. The legal and ethical claims are carried over as study material and were not updated.

### Q1: What circumstances constitute the practice of medicine?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Providing medical advice.
2. Treating patients.
3. Prescribing medication.
4. Prescribing eyeglasses.
5. Conducting or supervising childbirth.
6. Performing surgery.
---

### Q2: When may a license to practice medicine be revoked?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. A final judgment against the physician for a criminal offense.
2. The physician becomes addicted to drugs or alcohol.
3. The physician practices outside the authorized specialty.
4. The physician discloses patient secrets or neglects to preserve patient records.
5. The physician continues practicing despite being unable to do so.
---

### Q3: What violations are related to practicing medicine?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Practicing without a license.
2. A physician authorized to practice opens more than two clinics.
3. The physician fails to notify the Ministry of Health of the clinic’s location or a permanent change to it.
4. Medical equipment or supplies are held by someone who is not authorized to practice medicine.
---

### Q4: What circumstances invalidate a patient’s consent?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Consent to an unlawful procedure, such as criminal abortion or removal of a vital solitary organ.
2. Consent obtained through fraud or deception.
3. Consent obtained from someone who is not authorized to provide it.
---

### Q5: Give examples of medical practice undertaken for a purpose other than curing the patient.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Seeking profit.
2. Conducting medical experiments.
3. Performing an unlawful intervention.
---

### Q6: What are the two degrees of medical error?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Ordinary error.
2. Technical or professional error.
---

### Q7: What concerns may hinder disclosure of a medical error?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Fear for the physician’s reputation, job, and future.
2. Fear for the healthcare facility’s reputation.
3. Concern that disclosing the error will anger the patient and family.
---

### Q8: What factors contribute to medical errors?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Lack of accurate statistics on the number of medical errors and the resulting harm.
2. Lack of oversight by the Ministry of Health and the Medical Syndicate over medical institutions.
3. Inadequate checking of new physicians’ qualifications.
---

### Q9: What are the elements of medical liability?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. An error by the physician or a person working under the physician’s supervision.
2. Harm to the patient.
3. A causal relationship between the error and the harm.
---

### Q10: What are the types of legal liability?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Criminal.
2. Civil.
3. Administrative disciplinary.
4. Medical Syndicate disciplinary.
---

### Q11: What are the two types of injury medical reports?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Preliminary report.
2. Final medical report.
---

### Q12: What information should a medical report include?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. A description of the injury sustained by the person admitted for treatment.
2. The cause of the injury.
3. The date the injury occurred.
4. The time required for treatment.
---

### Q13: What forms may disciplinary sanctions against physicians take?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Reminder.
2. Warning.
3. Reprimand.
4. Professional fine of up to 1,000 Egyptian pounds, paid to the Medical Syndicate treasury.
5. Suspension for up to one year.
6. Removal from Medical Syndicate membership, which results in striking the physician’s name from Ministry of Health records.
---

### Q14: Give examples of deaths with a suspected cause.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Death suspected to result from a criminal cause, including burns, anesthesia or diagnostic error, negligence, abortion or complications, poisoning, or addiction.
2. Death during or immediately after surgery.
3. Death at work in a factory.
4. Death from epidemic diseases such as AIDS or meningitis.
5. Death resulting from transportation incidents.
---

### Q15: What steps should be taken before an autopsy?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. A physician must declare the patient dead.
2. A relative must be contacted and informed of the death.
3. If the death occurred in a hospital, it must be officially recorded in the hospital records.
4. The autopsy request must be complete and signed before the autopsy.
---

### Q16: What is meant by euthanasia?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** It means hastening the death of patients in a deep coma, patients considered incurable, or patients suffering from severe pain for which treatment is ineffective, purportedly to relieve pain and spare them a prolonged dying process.
---

### Q17: What are methods of euthanasia?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Removing life-support equipment or stopping life-sustaining measures such as feeding.
2. Giving the patient a poisonous drug.
3. Assisting the patient to kill themself.
---

### Q18: What steps are required before sex correction?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Hormonal testing.
2. Chromosomal analysis.
3. A period of psychological and hormonal treatment lasting at least two years.
---

### Q19: What measures must be taken during and after research involving human participants?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. The researcher must immediately stop any experiment if associated risks are found to exceed the expected benefits.
2. The researcher must provide each patient with the preventive, diagnostic, and treatment resources needed for the study.
3. The physician must obtain an undertaking from the research sponsor to provide the study drug to patients, establish its efficacy, and supply it without charge through the end of the treatment program.
4. Researchers are prohibited from conducting or participating in research that may involve confusion of lineage.
---

### Q20: What are the types of cloning?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Embryonic or reproductive.
2. Somatic or conventional.
3. Organ cloning.
---

### Q21: What is a surrogate uterus, and what is the Islamic Organization’s view of it?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** A surrogate uterus is a woman’s uterus, offered voluntarily or for payment, to carry an embryo created by fertilizing another woman’s egg with her husband’s sperm. The surrogate carries the pregnancy, and the egg provider receives the child.

The source states that the practice is absolutely prohibited because it involves a third party.
---

### Q22: How can the sex of a human embryo be controlled?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** At the couple’s request, a physician may stimulate the male-bearing sperm so the child will be male, or stimulate the female-bearing sperm so the child will be female.
---

### Q23: What religious rulings are given about controlling the sex of an embryo?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. It is not permissible at the level of the whole community.
2. It is permissible for an individual.
3. Some opinions prohibit it absolutely for fear that it could lead to one sex dominating the other.
---

### Q24: What is surgical contraception (sterilization), and what are the juristic views on it?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source refers to the department book, page 39, for the definition.

1. It is permissible for an individual when medically necessary, as determined by the physician.
2. It is absolutely impermissible at the level of the community.
---

### Q25: What is abortion?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** It is the expulsion of a pregnancy before completion. It may occur naturally because a woman is unable to carry the pregnancy, or it may be induced. Other terms include miscarriage, abortion, or expulsion.
---

### Q26: What is the only circumstance in which abortion is permitted?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** When the pregnancy threatens the mother’s life and abortion is the only means of averting that danger.
---

### Q27: What is hymenoplasty?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Repairing tears in the hymen or creating a new membrane.
2. Location: around the external vaginal opening.
3. Structure: two thin skin layers with loose tissue rich in blood vessels between them.
---

### Q28: What are the main causes of hymenal rupture before marriage?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Sexual intercourse.
2. An accident affecting the area.
---

### Q29: What are three methods for preserving skin in tissue banks?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Freezing, for up to 6 months.
2. Freeze-drying, for up to 18 months.
3. Refrigeration at 4°C above zero, for up to 3 weeks.
---

### Q30: What are the types of medical facilities?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Private medical clinic.
2. Specialty clinic.
3. Specialized medical center.
4. Private hospital.
5. Convalescent home.
---

### Q31: What rules should physicians follow when addressing the public about medical topics through the media or social media?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. Avoid stating the workplace, contact details, or praising personal experience or scientific achievements; state only the professional title and specialty.
2. Use simple language suitable for a non-specialist audience.
3. Avoid mentioning scientific opinions that are uncertain or not conclusively established.
---

### Q32: What is professional confidentiality?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** It includes everything a physician learns about a patient’s health and social circumstances, and anything the physician sees, hears, or understands about the patient or others during professional contact.
---

### Q33: When may a physician disclose professional secrets?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. At the patient’s request.
2. When disclosure benefits the patient.
3. When the physician is defending themself against an accusation of negligence, error, or malpractice.
4. For communicable diseases, by reporting to the nearest health office or preventive-health unit.
---

### Q34: Which communicable diseases must be reported?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Cholera, plague, tetanus, meningitis, rabies, tuberculosis, yellow fever, and sexually transmitted diseases including syphilis, gonorrhea, and AIDS.
---

### Q35: What four elements establish the crime of disclosing a professional secret?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** 1. An act of disclosure.
2. The disclosed information is a secret.
3. The disclosure is made by a physician or someone treated as one.
4. Criminal intent is present.
