### Q1 (Medical Ethics): A husband and wife consult a physician about delayed pregnancy. The physician performs investigations and discusses the wife's results with her. The husband later asks the physician whether the results show that his wife is infertile, but the physician refuses to disclose her information. Is the refusal appropriate? List four circumstances in which patient confidentiality may be disclosed.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source lists disclosure in cases such as communicable disease (for example, cholera or AIDS); certain death-related cases; when the patient requests disclosure; when the patient's condition is serious and disclosure is needed to provide care; when a physician must defend himself before a court; and when the physician is required to act as an expert witness. The source asks for four examples.

### Q2 (Medical Ethics): A television channel invites a prominent cardiologist to discuss heart disease and smoking. What rules should the physician follow when addressing the public through the media?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source says the physician should not identify the workplace or provide contact details, and should mention only the professional role and specialty. The physician should not promote medicines or participate in advertising, and should not diagnose or treat a patient through television without examining the patient.

### Q3 (Medical Ethics): A physician plans to study the effects of oral contraceptives on women's health and fertility. The Medical Ethics Committee asks the physician to state conditions for conducting the research. What conditions does the source list?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source lists that the research should have a therapeutic or scientific purpose; additional safeguards are required when participants cannot communicate effectively; written consent must be obtained from volunteers; and the research should be stopped if harmful effects appear.

### Q4 (Medical Ethics): In a lecture for new physicians, the speaker emphasizes avoiding unprofessional characteristics. Give examples listed in the source.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source lists disrespecting or undermining colleagues; cheating, falsification, or exploiting patients for gain; indifference toward patients and careless treatment; and dishonest financial or commercial dealings.

### Q5 (Medical Ethics): A 60-year-old patient with severe coronary artery disease needs urgent surgery. He refuses consent, although the physician explains the danger and the patient appears unable to make a decision. What should the physician do to protect the patient's interests?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Explain the treatment method, risks, and benefits clearly so the patient can make an informed decision. If the patient is distressed or unable to decide, the source says the physician may seek help from a colleague in discussing the treatment. Respect the patient's right to refuse and notify the hospital administration as described in the source.
