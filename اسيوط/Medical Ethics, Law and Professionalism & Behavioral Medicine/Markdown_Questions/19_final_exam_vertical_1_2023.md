### Q1 (Medical Ethics): A patient has severe right lower abdominal pain. An internist's tests indicate appendicitis, and the patient is referred to a surgeon, who confirms the diagnosis and begins preparations for surgery. An anesthesiologist is called to assess the patient. What are the anesthesiologist's duties?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Prepare the patient for anesthesia; perform the necessary investigations, review the patient's file, and assess suitability for anesthesia; ensure the injection equipment is sterile and the equipment is functioning; and supervise the patient during and after surgery until the patient regains consciousness.

### Q3 (Medical Ethics): A group of physicians complains that a hospital director lacks ethics, professionalism, and competence. What examples of unprofessional conduct does the source list?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Misusing authority; poor interactions with patients or colleagues; favoring one colleague or patient over another; allowing money to become the physician's main motivation; deceiving or misleading patients; failing to fulfill medical responsibilities toward patients; and conflicts of interest, including accepting company gifts or promoting one medicine for commercial gain.
**Source page:** 2

---

### Q4 (Medical Ethics): A physician plans research on a group of hospitalized patients but is unsure how to select participants. What conditions for selecting research participants does the source list?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Participants should have legal capacity; if a participant lacks capacity, consent must be obtained from a guardian. They must be fully informed about the research, its stages, goals, expected benefits, risks, and possible adverse effects. Participation must be voluntary, and participants may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without losing access to medical care. Financial gain should not be the primary reason for participation.
**Source page:** 2

### Q5 (Medical Ethics): A hospital plans training sessions on safety, prevention, and medical-waste disposal. Which groups are most at risk of exposure to infection from medical waste and should be prioritized?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Physicians, nurses, and other healthcare-facility workers; patients and then visitors; cleaning, laundry, and internal waste-transport staff; and workers at waste dumps or incinerators.
**Source page:** 3

### Q6 (Medical Ethics): During hernia surgery on a 7-year-old child, the surgeon accidentally cuts a major artery. A vascular surgeon is called and the injury is managed. Should the surgeon disclose the error, and why?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Yes. The source says disclosure can build trust between the patient and the healthcare system and promote justice in society.

### Q8 (Behavioral Medicine): Mention three uses of intelligence tests.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Educational purposes; vocational guidance and job selection; measuring intellectual deterioration in degenerative or infectious disease or central nervous system damage; assessing social and intellectual compatibility between spouses; and evaluating the effect of therapy on intellectual abilities.
