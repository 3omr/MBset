### Q47: Match the following vignettes with the appropriate defense mechanism:
1. A psychotherapist is irritated with a patient who consistently tries to put him down. Contrary to his usual punctuality, he seems unable to start this patient’s sessions on time.
2. A writer of mystery novels who has never had legal problems jokes about his ‘dark side’ and fantasies about leading an exciting life of crime.
3. A child is hit by his father for breaking a valuable vase. One hour later, he slaps his little sister for dropping his toy train.
4. A man cheats on his tax returns and justifies himself by thinking that the government wastes most of the tax money anyway.
5. A teacher dislikes one of her students but believes she should be impartial. She makes exaggerated efforts to be helpful and uncritical toward the student.

**Type:** Matching (kept as one grouped question).

**EXP:** 1. Psychotherapist vignette → Acting out; 2. Mystery writer vignette → Sublimation; 3. Child who hits his sister → Identification with the aggressor; 4. Tax-return vignette → Rationalization; 5. Teacher vignette → Reaction formation.
