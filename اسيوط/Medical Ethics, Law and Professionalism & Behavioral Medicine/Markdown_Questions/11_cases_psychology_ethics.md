# Source 11 — Psychology and ethics cases

Translated/normalized from the source. The PDF contains ten defense-mechanism cases, ten personality-disorder cases, twelve workplace-stress MCQs, and four image-only ethics cases with twelve subquestions. The twelve MCQ stems are each repeated on the following source page; one copy of each repeated item is retained. No answer key or marked MCQ choice was found, so those answers remain pending. The final four cases were recovered from the rendered pages with Arabic OCR and checked against the images; one subquestion refers to a lecture for its answer without providing it here.

### Q1: A 35-year-old man with ischemic heart disease says there is “nothing wrong” with him and that the diagnosis is an overreaction. Which defense mechanism is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Denial. The patient has difficulty accepting the illness and uses denial to cope with the news.

---

### Q2: A woman with an abnormal Pap smear calls herself useless and describes the result as deserved punishment for her past sexual behavior. Which defense mechanism is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Devaluation. The source defines this as an image-distorting defense in which a person attributes exaggerated negative qualities to themself when coping with conflict or stress.

---

### Q3: After arguing with her son, a physician is unusually forceful in advising her first patient of the day to lose weight. Which defense mechanism is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Displacement: transferring emotions from an unacceptable target to another person or object.

---

### Q4: A patient becomes shy, avoids eye contact, and giggles when discussing sexual history or undergoing a urogenital examination. Which defense mechanism does the source identify?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Fixation, described in the source as a partial or localized arrest at a more childish level of development.

---

### Q5: After her spouse dies of colon cancer, a woman develops abdominal pain and lethargy despite normal colonoscopy findings. Which defense mechanism does the source identify?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Identification: behavior is unconsciously patterned after another person; the source associates it with abuse or loss.

---

### Q6: A man who has affairs says he did so because his wife cared more about her career than him. Which defense mechanism is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Rationalization: reducing anxiety about socially unacceptable behavior by providing a logical-sounding reason for it.

---

### Q7: A 15-year-old reacts with intense hostility when his close male friend says he may be gay, and appears shaken while recounting their closeness. Which defense mechanism does the source identify?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Reaction formation: replacing an unacceptable feeling or idea with an emphasis on its opposite. The source interprets the patient's hostility as denying possible attraction to his friend.

---

### Q8: A woman with SLE begins bedwetting, throwing tantrums, and sucking her thumb while coping with painful dialysis. Which defense mechanism is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Regression, characterized by childlike behavior under stress, illness, or hospitalization.

---

### Q9: A witness to a violent crime cannot recall details about the assailant despite repeated attempts. Which defense mechanism does the source identify, and how does it distinguish suppression?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Repression is the involuntary exclusion of painful memories or impulses from awareness. Suppression is the conscious effort to exclude them without actually forgetting the event.

---

### Q10: A law student whose brother was killed by a drunk driver decides to become a criminal prosecutor despite his father's wishes. Which defense mechanism does the source identify?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Sublimation: channeling an unacceptable impulse into a socially acceptable action. The source describes the wish for vengeance through vigilantism as redirected into prosecution work.

---

### Q11: A young man has a history of repeated crimes, fights, animal abuse, inability to keep a job, impulsivity, and lack of remorse. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Antisocial personality disorder, characterized in the source by inability to conform to social norms and repetitive criminal behavior.

---

### Q12: A young man has few friends, fears new experiences and criticism, and avoids social activities despite being distressed by isolation. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Avoidant personality disorder. The source describes sensitivity to rejection, social withdrawal, and shyness, distinguishing it from schizoid personality disorder by noting that avoidant individuals are distressed by being alone.

---

### Q13: A patient tells her physician she is in love with him and threatens suicide when he recommends another physician. The source describes unstable mood, impulsivity, emptiness, and splitting. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Borderline personality disorder.

---

### Q14: A woman is insecure when alone, defers decisions to her domineering husband, and has difficulty asserting herself. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Dependent personality disorder, involving chronic excessive dependence, deference in decision-making, low self-esteem, and difficulty asserting oneself.

---

### Q15: A woman behaves seductively and dramatically, seeks attention, and overstates the closeness of her friendships. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Histrionic personality disorder.

---

### Q16: A man denies a myocardial infarction, boasts about wealth and influence, belittles staff, and expects special admiration. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Narcissistic personality disorder. The source describes denial of illness to protect fragile self-esteem, need for praise, lack of empathy, and entitlement.

---

### Q17: A man is perfectionistic, slow, meticulous, rigid about rules, and unable to enjoy recreation because he organizes it. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder.

---

### Q18: A man is hostile and guarded and insists that his physician deliberately prescribed harmful medication. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Paranoid personality disorder, characterized by suspicion and mistrust and interpreting others' motives as malevolent.

---

### Q19: A scientist lives alone, has no close relationships or hobbies, and is indifferent to having little social support. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Schizoid personality disorder, characterized by lifelong voluntary social withdrawal without psychosis.

---

### Q20: A woman reports that her hand disappeared, has an odd appearance and unusual beliefs, but no hallucinations or delusions. Which personality disorder is illustrated?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Schizotypal personality disorder, characterized by peculiar appearance, odd thoughts and behavior, and social isolation without psychosis.

### Q37: A patient with an injury after a fall is being prepared for surgery. The surgeon refers the patient for medical assessment but proceeds after obtaining only verbal consent. What should the surgeon's role have included?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The source says the surgeon should make the correct diagnosis, refer the patient to an internist for preoperative fitness assessment and investigations, and obtain written informed consent after explaining the operation's benefits and risks.

---

### Q38: In the same case, what error did the surgeon make?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** The surgeon did not obtain written informed consent.

---

### Q39: In the same case, what should the anesthesiologist do?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Assess fitness for surgery, obtain written consent, prepare the patient psychologically, check the anesthesia equipment and medicines and doses, and monitor vital signs during and after surgery until the patient wakes.

---

### Q40: What types of informed consent are listed for medical procedures?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Verbal and written consent. The source says to identify the situations in which each type is required.

### Q41: A child is brought by the parents to an emergency unit with severe pain. Examination identifies a kidney mass, and the case is referred for pediatric surgery and further treatment. What should the emergency physician do?

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Treat and diagnose the acute severe pain first, then refer the child to pediatric oncology or the relevant specialist for further assessment, biopsy, and surgery as indicated.
