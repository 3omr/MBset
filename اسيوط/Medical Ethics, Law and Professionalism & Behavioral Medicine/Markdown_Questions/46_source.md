### Q73: List barriers to patient participation in shared decision-making. The source provides eight blanks.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Lack of competence to practice shared decision-making; lack of communication skills; insufficient time to educate the patient; and the need for laws and policies that facilitate decision-making.

### Q74: List the six basic stages of shared decision-making between physician and patient.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Invite the patient to participate; provide information about benefits and risks; facilitate deliberation and decision-making; present available options; help the patient evaluate options according to their goals and values; and help the patient follow the decision.

---

### Q75: Complete the source definition of shared medical decision-making: It is a process of ______ for ______, through which ______ and ______ are chosen.

**Correct Answer:** -
**EXP:** Patients and physicians participate in decisions about diagnostic tests, treatment options, and care plans, and discuss the options available to the patient.
