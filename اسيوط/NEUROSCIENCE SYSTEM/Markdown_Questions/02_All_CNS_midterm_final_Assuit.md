### Q326: [18/12/2022, Physiology 3] Answer the questions about sound localization, macula functions, and scotopic versus photopic vision

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** key
**EXP:** Interaural time-delay detection circuit for sound localization in the horizontal plane: medial superior olivary neurons have a very short time constant, so temporal summation is poor; two signals, one from each ear, must arrive at exactly the same time to summate and allow the medial superior olive neuron to fire by spatial summation. Two functions of the human inner-ear macula: signaling orientation of the head in space during static head tilt forward, backward, or to one side in a fixed position; and maintenance of head and body posture relative to gravity while the body is still, such as during riding in a car or elevator with linear acceleration. Scotopic versus photopic vision, any two: scotopic vision is rod-mediated, has low visual acuity, poorly seen detail, absent colour vision, peripheral location, and works best at night or in darkness; photopic vision is cone-mediated, has high visual acuity, clearly seen detail, present colour vision, central location, and works best in sunlight or indoor light.
