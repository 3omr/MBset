### Q1: Compare typical and atypical antipsychotics regarding uses and adverse effects

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Typical antipsychotics are used mainly for positive psychotic symptoms and commonly cause extrapyramidal symptoms, hyperprolactinemia, and tardive dyskinesia. Atypical antipsychotics treat positive and negative symptoms and have a lower extrapyramidal risk but may cause weight gain, diabetes, dyslipidemia, and other metabolic effects. Clozapine may cause agranulocytosis and myocarditis

### Q38: Explain the role of metarhodopsin II in the excitation cascade of photoreceptors

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** Light converts retinal in rhodopsin to all-trans retinal and produces metarhodopsin II. Metarhodopsin II activates transducin, which activates phosphodiesterase. cGMP falls, cGMP-gated sodium and calcium channels close, the photoreceptor hyperpolarizes, and glutamate release decreases

### Q39: Compare the macula and crista ampullaris of the inner ear. Give three differences

**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**EXP:** The macula lies in the utricle and saccule and detects linear acceleration and static head position. Its sensory epithelium is covered by an otolithic membrane containing otoconia. The crista ampullaris lies in the ampulla of a semicircular duct and detects angular acceleration. Its hair bundles project into a gelatinous cupula without otoconia
