### Q13: Discuss glutamate receptors and mention which one is mostly implicated in the development of schizophrenia

### Q14: Describe glutamate-glutamine cycle and mention its importance to glutamatergic neurotransmission
**Correct Answer:** -
**Answer Source:** derived
**Tag:** Department, GDs, Biochemistry GD 2
**Year:** None
**EXP:** Neuronal glutamate is released into the synapse and taken up by astrocytes, where glutamine synthetase converts it to glutamine. Astrocytes export glutamine to neurons, and neuronal glutaminase reconverts it to glutamate. This recycles transmitter, replenishes neuronal stores and limits extracellular glutamate excitotoxicity.
